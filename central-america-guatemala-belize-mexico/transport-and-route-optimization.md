# Transport & route optimization

## Geographic conclusion

The efficient direction is:

**Guatemala highlands → Petén → western Belize → Belize coast → southern/eastern Yucatán → Cancún**

Do not reverse the route unless a future fare search produces a large airfare advantage.

## Route corrections

### 1. Skip Lake Atitlán → Guatemala City backtracking

In the full route, continue from Atitlán toward Lanquín/Semuc. Returning to Guatemala City only to go north wastes distance.

Exception: the **60:40 route** deliberately returns toward GUA because the **GUA→FRS flight** saves more time than the backtrack costs.

### 2. Belize City is a transport node

San Ignacio→Belize City→Caye Caulker fits as one travel day.

Only sleep in Belize City for a specific history/museum goal or because a connection fails.

### 3. Bacalar is a transport optimization, not filler

Rome2Rio planning comparisons:
- Caye Caulker→Valladolid: roughly 10 hours by ferry/bus chain.
- Caye Caulker→Bacalar: fastest route overview roughly 5h12.
- Bacalar→Valladolid: roughly 4h44 by bus.

The full route gets a real lagoon destination while splitting the worst transfer.

### 4. Valladolid beats Cancún as the Chichén base

Valladolid is around 45 km from Chichén Itzá and close to Ek Balam. It is a better cultural base and lets you reach Chichén closer to opening before Cancún day-trip volume.

### 5. The 60:40 route changes the transport economics

For 28 days, road/ferry travel is worthwhile because intermediate stops add value.

For ~12 days:
- use GUA→FRS flight;
- use BZE→CUN flight;
- remove Semuc and Bacalar.

That is a different optimization objective: sightseeing value per day rather than minimum cash cost.

## Border-risk rules

- Never put a non-refundable major tour immediately after an international land crossing.
- Carry accessible proof of onward travel/accommodation when required.
- Re-check entry/exit fees and immigration forms close to departure.
- Belize/Mexico sea+land combinations are connection-sensitive; do not rely on a headline duration with zero buffer.

## Fatigue rules

- Acatenango gets a recovery/weather buffer in the 28-day route.
- Semuc→Flores is a transfer day, not a sightseeing day.
- Reef excursions get a weather buffer in the full route.
- Do not combine Semuc sightseeing + Semuc→Flores in one day just to save a night.
