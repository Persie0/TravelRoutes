# Guatemala → Belize → Mexico — February 2027

Research snapshot: **2026-08-23**. This route is deliberately optimized for **almost the entire month of February 2027**, with **1–28 February** as the reference trip.

> Flight prices below are indicative Skyscanner observations, not locked fares. Transport durations are Rome2Rio planning estimates, not guaranteed schedules. Re-check live flights, local timetables, border rules, opening hours and volcanic/weather conditions before booking.

## Recommended route

**Europe → Guatemala City → Antigua → Acatenango/Fuego → Lake Atitlán → Semuc Champey → Flores/Tikal/Yaxhá → San Ignacio → Belize City transit → Caye Caulker → Bacalar → Valladolid/Chichén Itzá/Ek Balam → Cancún → Europe**

This is the route I would book for a full-February trip.

### The important optimizations

1. **Do not return from Lake Atitlán to Guatemala City.** Continue north/east toward Lanquín/Semuc Champey by direct tourist shuttle/private transfer. The Guatemala City return is pure backtracking.
2. **Do not waste a night in Belize City unless you actively want to see it.** Use it as the transfer point between San Ignacio and the Caye Caulker ferry.
3. **Break Caye Caulker → Valladolid at Bacalar.** Rome2Rio shows the direct ferry+bus journey at roughly 10 hours. Bacalar turns the ugly transfer into two useful travel legs while adding one of southern Quintana Roo's strongest sights.
4. **Base yourself in Valladolid for Chichén Itzá, not Cancún.** Valladolid is much closer, has its own sights and cenotes, and lets you arrive at Chichén near opening time.
5. **Keep Cancún short.** It is excellent as a beach/flight endpoint, but it is not the reason to devote several days to this itinerary.
6. **For February 1–28, skip Mexico City in the main route.** It is a great destination, but current indicative fares make it ~€100+ more expensive, and it costs 2–3 days that are better used on this route. A Mexico City variant is documented below.

## Why February is unusually good

February is close to ideal for the combination of activities here:

- Guatemala highlands: dry-season conditions improve the odds of clear Acatenango/Fuego views.
- Semuc Champey/Petén: generally drier than the wet-season months, making road/trail logistics easier.
- Belize: dry-season timing is favorable for Cayo adventures and reef days.
- Caribbean/Yucatán: outside the official Atlantic hurricane season (June 1–November 30).
- Valladolid/Chichén Itzá: warm but generally less punishing than late spring.

The main February downside is **cold/wind at altitude on Acatenango**. Pack for genuinely cold overnight conditions even though the rest of the trip is tropical.

## February flight optimization — Vienna vs Budapest

### Raw-fare winner for Feb 1–28

Indicative Skyscanner snapshot:

- **BUD → GUA, Feb 1: ~€453**
- **CUN → BUD, Feb 28: ~€358**
- raw long-haul total: **~€811**

### Best practical compromise if starting from Vienna

- **VIE → GUA, Feb 1: ~€472**
- **CUN → BUD, Feb 28: ~€358**
- raw long-haul total: **~€830**
- only one Budapest positioning journey, at the end.

That costs only ~€19 more in airfare than BUD both ways, so it can easily win once the first Vienna→Budapest transfer, luggage, food and buffer time are included.

### Vienna-only comparison

The February data observed:

- VIE → GUA, Feb 1: ~€472.
- CUN → VIE, Feb 26: ~€400.
- total ~€872, but it shortens the route to 26 days.

CUN → VIE was ~€367 on Feb 24, but a Feb 1–24 trip defeats the goal of using almost the whole month.

### Recommendation

For **Feb 1–28**, search in this order immediately before booking:

1. **VIE→GUA Feb 1 + CUN→BUD Feb 28** — current practical favorite.
2. **BUD→GUA Feb 1 + CUN→BUD Feb 28** — current raw-fare winner.
3. VIE→GUA + CUN→VIE with dates as close to Feb 1/28 as possible.
4. BUD→GUA + CUN→VIE if a surprise fare appears.

Do not optimize a €20 airfare difference at the cost of an extra positioning journey unless the Budapest connection is genuinely convenient.

## Mexico City: worth adding?

**As a destination: yes. For this specific Feb 1–28 optimization: no, unless you value Mexico City more than 2–3 route days.**

Indicative February observations:

- CUN → Mexico City/NLU: from ~€38 on selected dates.
- MEX → BUD, Feb 26: ~€424.
- compared with CUN → BUD, Feb 28 at ~€358.

A workable variant is therefore to reach Cancún by Feb 23, fly CUN→NLU/MEX, spend ~2–3 nights in Mexico City and fly MEX→BUD Feb 26. On observed fares this is roughly **€100+ extra** on the return side and gives up the final two February days.

Keep it as an optional variant, not the default.
