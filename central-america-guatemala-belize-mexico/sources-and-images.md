# Sources, articles & visual references

## Route / transport tools

### Rome2Rio

Used to compare route geometry and approximate modes/durations for:

- Guatemala City → Antigua
- Antigua → Panajachel
- Lake Atitlán → Semuc Champey
- Semuc Champey → Flores
- Flores → Tikal
- Flores → San Ignacio
- San Ignacio → Belize City
- Belize City → Caye Caulker
- Caye Caulker → Valladolid
- Caye Caulker → Bacalar
- Bacalar → Valladolid
- Valladolid → Cancún

Main site: https://www.rome2rio.com/

**Caveat:** route-overview times are not guaranteed schedules. The Atitlán search in particular sometimes resolved Panajachel to a specific property, so use it for mode comparison and re-check local shuttles once dates are fixed.

## Chichén Itzá — INAH official

Main INAH place page:
https://lugares.inah.gob.mx/es/node/4335

INAH archaeology directory:
https://lugares.inah.gob.mx/es/zonas-arqueologicas

World Heritage record/heritage context:
https://lugares.inah.gob.mx/es/node/6131

### Special Serie Inicial information

INAH published a 2026 note about reservations for the **Grupo de Serie Inicial** (a specific additional area), including limited Thu–Sat access windows and advance reservation instructions:
https://inah.gob.mx/images/zonas/yucatan/chichenitza/Serie%20Inicial%20Informacion%202026.pdf

Do not confuse that special-area reservation procedure with general Chichén Itzá entry; verify current rules for your travel year.

## Ek Balam — INAH

INAH archaeology directory (filter/search Ek' Balam):
https://lugares.inah.gob.mx/es/zonas-arqueologicas

INAH describes walls, murals, stelae, plazas, palaces and the richly decorated Acropolis.

# Weather / seasonal sources

## Belize National Meteorological Service

Climate summary:
https://nms.gov.bz/climate-services/climate-summary/

Key route-planning information:
- most rainfall June–November;
- dry season roughly November–May;
- cooler/frontal dry-season period Nov–Feb;
- warmer dry period Mar–May.

## NOAA / National Hurricane Center

Atlantic tropical cyclone climatology:
https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/climo/

Mariner's tropical cyclone climatology guide:
https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/marinersguide.pdf

Key planning information:
- Atlantic hurricane season: June 1–November 30;
- sharp climatological peak around September;
- most activity concentrated around mid-August through mid-October.
