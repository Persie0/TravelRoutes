## Why this order wins

1. **No rental car in Faro/Lisbon/Coimbra/Porto:** trains/buses are faster and parking is a nuisance.
2. **Car begins exactly where it becomes valuable:** Minho/Galicia/Asturias/Cantabria coast, Picos, small villages and beaches.
3. **No coast backtracking after Bilbao:** return inland via Burgos–León–Bragança–Douro, turning the necessary return drive into new sightseeing.
4. **Picos gets two nights/one real hiking day:** northern weather is too variable to design a one-shot mountain visit.
5. **As Catedrais is placed in September with a reservation/tide warning:** access authorization is required from July 1–September 30 and beach-level access is tide-dependent.
