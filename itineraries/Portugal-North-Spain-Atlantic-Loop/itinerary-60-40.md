# 60:40 highlights itinerary — 12 days

Goal: keep roughly the highest 60% of experience value in ~40% of the month.

| Day | Sleep | Core experience |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Lagos | Fly Faro; transfer Lagos; Ponta da Piedade sunset |
| 2 | Lisbon | Lagos cliffs/beach AM; bus Lisbon |
| 3 | Lisbon | Lisbon historic core + Belém |
| 4 | Porto | Early Sintra essentials; late northbound transfer to Porto |
| 5 | Porto | Porto + Ribeira/Gaia; pick car late |
| 6 | Santiago | Porto → Vigo coast → Santiago |
| 7 | Santiago | Santiago + compact Costa da Morte taste if energy allows |
| 8 | Arenas de Cabrales | Fast Galicia coast via As Catedrais → Picos base |
| 9 | Potes | Ruta del Cares **or** Covadonga/Lakes depending weather |
| 10 | Bilbao | Potes → Santander coast → Bilbao |
| 11 | León | Bilbao + Guggenheim AM; Gaztelugatxe optional; inland to León |
| 12 | Porto | León → Douro → Porto; fly next morning if needed |

## Full route vs 60:40

| Place | Full | 60:40 | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Faro/Ria Formosa | Yes | transit only | Lagos is higher-value coast per hour |
| Lagos/Ponta da Piedade | Yes | **Yes** | iconic Algarve geology + beach |
| Lisbon | Yes | **Yes** | unique urban/cultural density |
| Sintra | Yes | **Yes, compressed** | exceptionally high uniqueness |
| Coimbra | Yes | No | excellent, but lower marginal value on a 12-day sprint |
| Porto | Yes | **Yes** | high-value city + natural rental-car handoff point |
| Cíes | Yes | No | superb but ferry consumes a full day |
| Santiago | Yes | **Yes** | unique pilgrimage city |
| Costa da Morte/A Coruña | Yes | compressed | scenery retained, city night cut |
| As Catedrais | Yes | **Yes** | distinctive geology directly on route |
| Oviedo/Cudillero | Yes | drive-through/skip | attractive but substitutable |
| Picos de Europa | Yes | **Yes** | highest-value nature/hiking section |
| Santander/Santillana | Yes | compressed | scenic connector, not core |
| Bilbao | Yes | **Yes** | Guggenheim + Basque city/food |
| Burgos/León/Douro return | Yes | León+Douro | turns return drive into worthwhile stops |

### Biggest sacrifices
Cíes Islands, a proper Costa da Morte day, A Coruña, Cudillero/Oviedo depth, Santander recovery day and Coimbra. These are all good; they simply lose on value-per-transfer-hour against Lagos, Lisbon/Sintra, Porto, Santiago, Picos and Bilbao.
