# Speedrun itinerary — Faro → Portugal → Northern Spain → Porto

## Goal

Keep essentially all important S/A-tier city, island, coast and mountain experiences while compressing stays to the minimum useful time.

- Full route: 30 days
- 60:40: ~12 days, major regions cut
- **Speedrun theoretical minimum: ~19 days**
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum: 21 days**

The extra two recommended days protect Picos weather, Cíes ferry capacity, As Catedrais tide timing and long Atlantic-coast driving days.

## Important experiences retained

Faro/Ria Formosa, Lagos/Ponta da Piedade, Lisbon, Sintra, Coimbra, Porto, Douro, Minho coast, Cíes, Santiago, Costa da Morte, A Coruña/Tower of Hercules, As Catedrais, Cudillero/Oviedo, Covadonga/Picos, Ruta del Cares, Cantabria, Bilbao/Guggenheim, Gaztelugatxe, León and the inland Douro return.

## Speedrun rules

1. Use public transport until Porto; the car starts only where the coastal road trip begins.
2. Book Cíes, As Catedrais and Gaztelugatxe first because fixed access can break the compressed schedule.
3. As Catedrais must be timed to usable low tide, not merely assigned to a calendar morning.
4. Keep one flexible day for Picos weather; if the hike fails, spend it before continuing east.
5. Avoid adding beach-rest days unless weather forces them; the Speedrun is sightseeing-first.

**Speedrun removes recovery days and duplicate city nights, not the Atlantic coast/Picos/Basque spine.**
