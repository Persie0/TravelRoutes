- Static maps generated locally: Basemap/GSHHS coastlines and international borders.
- Interactive map: Leaflet + OpenStreetMap tiles (attribution included in HTML).
- GeoJSON: WGS84 lon/lat.
- Route lines are planning connectors between verified stops, not turn-by-turn road geometry.
