# Flights — September 2027 strategy

## Important limitation
As of **23 August 2026**, Skyscanner's month-level search tool does **not yet accept September 2027** because it is more than one year ahead. Therefore no 2027 fare below is presented as bookable/current.

## Best structural strategy
**Open-jaw:** **Vienna (VIE) → Faro (FAO)** outbound, **Porto (OPO) → Vienna (VIE)** home.

This exactly matches the land route and avoids wasting a day returning to Faro or Lisbon.

## Raw cheapest vs total value
If BUD saves only €10–30, Vienna usually wins for an Austrian starting point once train/bus positioning, luggage, extra time and missed-connection risk are included. Budapest becomes interesting only if the **total** saving is substantial (roughly €60+ per person after positioning, or much better flight times/directness).
