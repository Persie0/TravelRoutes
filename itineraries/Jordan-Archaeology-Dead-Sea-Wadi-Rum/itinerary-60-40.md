# 60:40 — Jordan highlights in 11 days

> **Safety gate — research date 19 Nov 2026.** Austria's foreign ministry assigns **level 3** to most of Jordan (advises against nonessential travel) and **level 4** to regions close to Syria and Iraq. Regional conflict may disrupt flights. **Do not treat the route as currently cleared for a holiday.** Recheck [BMEIA](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/jordanien) before any nonrefundable booking, seek insurance confirmation, and omit border-adjacent Umm Qais / Umm al-Jimal if the relevant warning remains. This example assumes conditions improve.

**Target:** ≈60% of normalized experienced value using **11 of 28 days (39%)**, not 60% of the list of stops. The subjective scoring in [sights.csv](data/sights.csv) weights priority and two 10-point measures; the goal is a heuristic, not a scientifically measured 60% satisfaction guarantee.

| Jerash | full day | 3–4 h | Roman ruins are must-do |

| Madaba + Mount Nebo | long day | half-to-full day | Geographically aligned |
| Dana | 2+ days | short viewpoint stop | Hike sacrifices to protect Petra time |
| Karak | 1 day | 1–2 h | Same highway corridor |

| Feynan 14km trek, Petra third day | several days | cut | Beautiful but time-intensive |

### What this version deliberately loses

The northern Hellenistic/Gadara specialty sites, Qusayr Amra's unique frescoes, some Byzantine mosaics, the full Wadi Dana trek and Petra's long alternative paths. For archaeology-focused travelers choose the 14-day Speedrun instead.
