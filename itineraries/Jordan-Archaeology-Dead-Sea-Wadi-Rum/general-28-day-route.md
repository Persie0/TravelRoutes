# Best 14–16-day first-time Jordan route

**Default recommendation: 15 days in October or April.** In Jordan the true experience-value curve saturates before day 28.

| Day(s) | Base | Keep |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Amman | Citadel, Roman theatre, markets, Jordan Museum |
| 3 | Amman | Jerash and Ajloun Castle |
| 4 | Amman or As-Salt | As-Salt; optional Umm Qais / Pella only if border security situation permits |
| 5 | Madaba | Bethany baptism site, Mount Nebo, Madaba mosaics |
| 6 | Dead Sea | Half day floating and recovery |
| 7 | Karak | Wadi Mujib Siq in season, Karak Castle |
| 8 | Dana | Scenic King's Highway, Dana village trails |
| 9 | Wadi Musa | Shobak and Little Petra / Beidha |
| 10–11 | Wadi Musa | Two full Petra days (Treasury, Royal Tombs, Monastery, viewpoints) |
| 12–13 | Wadi Rum | Guided 4x4 desert loop and camp night; optional hiking |
| 14 | Aqaba | Snorkeling/reef or city |
| 15 | Departure | Drive Aqaba → AMM, or position closer to airport evening 14 |

For **16 days**, use Day 16 for weather, a third Petra day or a second Red Sea day. For **14 days**, compress Amman city highlights into arrival day and cut the extra As-Salt day.

**Order justification:** north cluster before descending the Dead Sea escarpment; then one coherent southbound King's Highway section; Petra before desert to avoid a short, exhausted Petra visit; Aqaba only after Wadi Rum; the final return via Desert Highway is the unavoidable repositioning to Amman when Aqaba international flights are not competitive.

**Not first-trip essential:** a dedicated Azraq night, multiple Dana days, a third Aqaba day, and all seven UNESCO sites. Northern border-adjacent sites should never be forced when warnings remain.
