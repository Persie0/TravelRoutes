# Material itinerary variants

> **Safety gate — research date 19 Nov 2026.** Austria's foreign ministry assigns **level 3** to most of Jordan (advises against nonessential travel) and **level 4** to regions close to Syria and Iraq. Regional conflict may disrupt flights. **Do not treat the route as currently cleared for a holiday.** Recheck [BMEIA](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/jordanien) before any nonrefundable booking, seek insurance confirmation, and omit border-adjacent Umm Qais / Umm al-Jimal if the relevant warning remains. This example assumes conditions improve.

## A. Archaeology-first (16–19 days)

Add Quseir Amra frescoes, Umm ar-Rasas mosaic, Gadara/Pella if the border-specific warning permits, 3 consecutive Petra days, As-Salt and Madaba museum time. Cut 2 Aqaba days and one Wadi Rum hiking day. Best for Roman, Byzantine, Nabataean and Islamic layers.

## B. Hiking and geology (16–21 days)

Dana reserve 3 days including Wadi Dana→Feynan with guide and vehicle exit; Petra 3 days (High Place and Monastery); Wadi Rum 3 days with guided ridge/scramble hikes. October or April. **No winter wadi canyons in heavy rain.**

## C. Budget with no rental car (10–14 days)

JETT Amman→Jerash day trip, Amman→Petra, Petra→Wadi Rum (confirm JETT timings), Wadi Rum→Aqaba, Aqaba→Amman. Dead Sea by organized day transport, a taxi or day pass. **Cuts:** As-Salt/Pella/Umm Rasas/Dana and eastern castles. Works only if operators run on exact days.

## D. Coastal/reef variant (14–18 days)

Add 2–4 Aqaba days for snorkeling and scuba. Certified diving requires observing a **no-fly interval** following diving according to operator and dive agency guidance; do not dive the last morning before a flight.

## E. Flights and reverse route

If affordable AMM arrival / AQJ departure emerges, finish in Aqaba and avoid 3–5h northward road positioning. No matched-date AQJ sample found in the November 2026 indicative search, so **no unverified booking claim**. Reverse the itinerary only if the price/timetable clearly wins after positioning.

## F. Relaxed 12–15d

Skip Azraq, Umm Qais, Pella, Feynan, and Mukawir; keep Amman, Jerash, Madaba/Nebo, Dead Sea/Mujib, Dana, 2-day Petra, Rum 1–2 nights, Aqaba day. This is the recommended first-time experience if future travel safety improves.
