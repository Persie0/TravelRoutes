# Comprehensive 28-day Jordan itinerary

> **Safety gate — research date 19 Nov 2026.** Austria's foreign ministry assigns **level 3** to most of Jordan (advises against nonessential travel) and **level 4** to regions close to Syria and Iraq. Regional conflict may disrupt flights. **Do not treat the route as currently cleared for a holiday.** Recheck [BMEIA](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/jordanien) before any nonrefundable booking, seek insurance confirmation, and omit border-adjacent Umm Qais / Umm al-Jimal if the relevant warning remains. This example assumes conditions improve.

This is the **completeness / slow-travel** version required by the TravelRoutes template. For a normal first-time trip, 14–16 days is more efficient. Plan in **October** when Wadi Mujib is scheduled to be in season, and book all desert/hiking operators with weather flexibility. Northern border-adjacent areas and eastern desert loop remain conditional on official travel advice.

## Why this version takes 28 days

- Ten or more days go to lower-intensity exploration, reserve trails, extra Petra hiking, Aqaba coast and environmental/weather buffers.
- The route never compresses Petra into a rushed day trip or Wadi Rum into an afternoon.
- Some places (Umm Qais, Pella, eastern desert) have markedly lower value-per-transfer-hour, and may be omitted due to security advice. The 11-day and 14-day variants are better for most travelers.
- **Day 23 and consecutive Petra tickets:** the 3-day Petra pass should cover days **21–23**, not day 20. Day 20 is Little Petra outside the main park. Confirm back-trail admission and guided access.
- **Feynan logistics:** no general through-road is promised; book the canyon lodge pickup/return and luggage transfer before attempting the long trek.
- **Day 28:** if the flight leaves early, sleep at Amman/airport on night 27, rather than depend on the same-day 4h drive from Aqaba. Reallocate Day 27 as transfer.

## Backup logic

- Wadi Mujib closed: retain the Dead Sea half-day and put the time into Karak / Dana.
- Petra storms / rare snow: use a free buffer day for safe reopening; never enter closed wadis.
- Unfavorable safety notice: do not execute the holiday itinerary; the written route is conditional planning.
