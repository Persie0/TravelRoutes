## Prebooking order

1. **Do not buy non-refundable flights** while BMEIA level 3 advice remains unless conscious of travel risks and insurance exclusions.
2. When travel clears, compare direct return vs one-way/open-jaw; confirm 14-night schedule, baggage and BUD positioning.
3. Obtain official [Jordan Pass](https://jordanpass.jo/contents/Prices.aspx) Explorer (75 JOD / two consecutive Petra days) and verify relevant visa procedure; buy before arrival if eligible.
4. Reserve Wadi Musa base close enough for 06:00-ish Petra entrance and a licensed Wadi Rum camp jeep package.
5. Book rental car from AMM or a driver, confirm insurance and one-way terms; **do not drive into the protected desert** with a standard hire vehicle.
6. Check [Petra official opening hours](https://ticket.gov.jo/en/opening-hours), [RSCN canyon operations](https://www.experiencejordan.com/wadi-mujib-siq-trail-guide/), and [JETT live schedules](https://jett.com.jo/en/live-schedule) a few days before travel.
