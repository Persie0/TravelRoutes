# Month-by-month weather / activity suitability

Ordinal **1–10** scores below are **editorial judgments**, not instrument readings or predictions. They rank comfortable outdoor archaeology, strenuous hiking, water-trail opening, and sea activity, not only temperature.

**Best overall:** October, then April. **Best-value compromise:** late March / early November (caveat: Wadi Mujib typically closed outside Apr–Oct). September still hot. **Avoid** June–August for long Petra and Wadi Rum walks; winter can bring cold/snow in highlands and wadis can flash-flood after rain.

**October drawback:** high demand and potential first autumn storms; check canyon day-by-day. **April drawback:** some spring storms and early-month trail opening uncertainty. Daylight is shorter in October; verify official Petra opening hours [here](https://ticket.gov.jo/en/opening-hours), not travel-blog blanket '06:00–18:00'. The [Jordan Tourism Board](https://international.visitjordan.com/where-to-go/) and [Wadi Mujib guide](https://www.experiencejordan.com/wadi-mujib-siq-trail-guide/) describe seasonality.

### Speedrun weather risk

Highest: Wadi Mujib canyon closure (weather-dependent, lifejacket/water required, min age 18), then Petra slippery stairs/flash floods, followed by Wadi Rum sand and heat. Put movable buffer before a nonrefundable Wadi Rum night. Monitor RSCN operations shortly before visiting.

### Packing

Sun protection, hiking shoes, sandals/water shoes for Dead Sea/Mujib, layers for cool desert night, electrolytes/water and waterproof phone pouch. Do not swim with open wounds, dive under the hypersaline Dead Sea surface or allow saltwater in eyes.
