# Jordan Speedrun — all core S / essentially all A sights

> **Safety gate — research date 19 Nov 2026.** Austria's foreign ministry assigns **level 3** to most of Jordan (advises against nonessential travel) and **level 4** to regions close to Syria and Iraq. Regional conflict may disrupt flights. **Do not treat the route as currently cleared for a holiday.** Recheck [BMEIA](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/jordanien) before any nonrefundable booking, seek insurance confirmation, and omit border-adjacent Umm Qais / Umm al-Jimal if the relevant warning remains. This example assumes conditions improve.

**Theoretical minimum: ~12 full ground days** with unusually good arrivals and no closures. **Recommended speedrun minimum: 14 days**. It is *longer than the 60:40 route* because it protects the second-tier A-sites instead of deleting them.

## Recommended 14-day timed sequence

**Why not 10 days?** You could do 10–11 days only by deleting substantial A-tier destinations (Umm Qais, As-Salt, Quseir Amra, Bethany, Um er-Rasas, Dana trails) or reducing Petra/Red Sea time to unsafe or poor-quality slivers. That's a *60:40 route*, not a Speedrun.

### Failure handling

One **floating contingency half-day** belongs first to Wadi Mujib (flash flood closure); second to Petra (rain or heat); third to Wadi Rum (wind/sand). Do not enter a closed canyon to keep a speedrun. An early flight in Amman requires transfer to airport / Amman **the night before**. In the current 2026 security climate, **Umm Qais and eastern sites are omitted unless conditions improve**; their omission is a safety adjustment, not a scored optimization.
