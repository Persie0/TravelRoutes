### Cheapest raw airfare

**Budapest → Amman → Budapest** is cheapest among quoted matched-duration flights: **€152 for 1–15 April 2027 (14 nights)**, Wizz Air direct, not including positioning/luggage. An October short stay shows **€145 for 5–12 Oct** but **cannot be generalized to a 14-day October itinerary**.

### Best total-value strategy

A **Vienna–Amman round trip** is the simpler/default-value flight if Budapest positioning, baggage, and flight arrival/departure slots exceed roughly **€129** additional total. April matched 14-day sample: Vienna €281 vs Budapest €152, gross difference €129. With a cheap bus/rail to BUD and minimal luggage, Budapest may still win. Prices/times for positioning were not live-quoted, so there is no invented precise door-to-door winner.

### Other tested route shells

- VIE→AQJ return and BUD→AQJ return October 2027: the indicative engine returned no sample prices. This **does not establish** no service.
- AQJ departure / Amman arrival open-jaw: strategically attractive after Aqaba, but no independently confirmed matched-date fare; compare manually, not assumed cheaper.
- Reverse the route if a cheaper AQJ arrival + AMM departure appears; the sightseeing order remains reasonable after reversal.

- To assess total cost include BUD/VIE positioning, airport-to-city, baggage, and return buffer hotel where relevant. Never compare a 7-night ticket against a 15-night trip as if equivalent.
