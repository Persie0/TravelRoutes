## Sight clusters and caveats

**Petra** is a sprawling city, not only the Treasury; the Monastery is a considerable climb. Choose 2 full days minimum, 3 for alternate ridge hikes. Consecutive-day ticket rules apply. **Jerash** is the main monumental Roman stop. Amman's theatre + citadel make a strong urban Roman archaeology pair. **Umm Qais** and Pella add specialist depth but northern border risk changes their practicality.

**Quseir Amra**, **Umm ar-Rasas**, **As-Salt**, **Petra**, **Wadi Rum**, **Bethany**, and **Umm al-Jimal** appear on the UNESCO Jordan list; archaeological inclusion is not a safety endorsement.

**Wadi Mujib** operates seasonally and is excluded from Jordan Pass; prior-source operator guidance states April 1–Oct 31 subject to water/weather, minimum age 18, and 2–3h duration. **Aqaba coral** is a high-uniqueness supplement if the marine weather cooperates. Do not step on living coral.
