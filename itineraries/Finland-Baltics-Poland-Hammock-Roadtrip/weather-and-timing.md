# Weather and Timing

## Best choice: August
The route spans Arctic Lapland plus Baltic/Polish cities. **August is the best compromise**: usable hammock temperatures, long daylight, fewer insects as the month progresses, and the Baltic coast is still in summer mode.

Visit Finland notes that most mosquitoes have disappeared by late summer, while late August begins to show the first ruska colours. September gives stronger ruska and aurora potential, but official guidance gives Lapland September temperatures roughly **0–10°C**, with nights that can be much colder.

## Best windows
- **Best overall:** 1–31 August.
- **Best hybrid summer + first dark nights:** ~10 August–7 September.
- **Best scenery if hammock comfort is secondary:** ~3–28 September, with a warmer sleep system.
- **Best midnight-sun version:** late June to mid-July, but bugs and crowds increase.

## Why not pure September for this exact request?
If sleeping in hotels, September could arguably win because ruska + aurora are exceptional. With a hammock, the risk of cold wet nights materially changes the score. A 0°C-comfort quilt/underquilt can make it viable, but the trip stops being casual summer camping.
