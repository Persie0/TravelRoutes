# Transport and Route Optimization

## Finland: rail first, car only in Lapland
### Helsinki → Oulu / Rovaniemi
Rome2Rio research snapshot (2026-08-24): Helsinki→Oulu train about **5 h 28 min**, drive about **7 h**, flight door-to-door around **3 h 19 min**. Rovaniemi→Helsinki train about **8 h 17 min**, driving about **9 h 45 min**. The night train therefore converts a long transfer into accommodation and protects daylight.

**Recommendation:** fly to Helsinki if airfare is good; spend 1–2 days; take a sleeper to Rovaniemi and rent there. Oulu is an **optional fare/route stop**, not mandatory sightseeing. If a VIE/BUD→OUL fare is dramatically cheaper, use Oulu as the rail gateway and continue north; otherwise skip it.

### Why not rent in Helsinki?
Helsinki→northern Lapland by car consumes huge mileage before the route reaches the highest-value nature. The car becomes valuable from Rovaniemi east/north: Oulanka, Riisitunturi, Pyhä-Luosto, Saariselkä and Inari.

### Finland rental geometry
Best default: **Rovaniemi round-trip rental**. One-way Oulu→Rovaniemi/Ivalo can save time, but only if the quoted relocation fee is modest. A round-trip loop is simpler and avoids unknown drop charges.

## Helsinki → Tallinn
Rome2Rio snapshot: ferry roughly **2 h 15 min**; flying is not worth the airport overhead. Use the ferry.

## Baltics: two viable strategies
### A. Nature-first: Tallinn → Vilnius one-way car
Route: Tallinn → Lahemaa → Pärnu → Riga → Gauja → Kuldīga → Klaipėda/Nida → Kaunas → Trakai → Vilnius.

Why it works: forests, bogs, beaches, small towns and park trailheads are exactly where a car creates value.

**Cost warning:** international one-way fees are real. Current examples found in 2026 terms include about **€115** Riga↔Tallinn/Vilnius on Europcar Latvia, **€170–220** on Avis Latvia, and a BalticCar example of **€265 Tallinn→Vilnius**. Sixt Estonia says Baltic-country driving is Zone 0, but international one-way is on request. Always quote the exact rental before locking the route.

### B. Budget/solo: buses between capitals + local rentals
- Tallinn→Riga: Rome2Rio bus about **4 h 50 min**.
- Riga→Vilnius: bus about **3 h 40 min**, train around **4 h 6 min**.
- Rent 1–2 days around Tallinn for Lahemaa; 1–2 days around Riga for Gauja; use train/bus in Lithuania.

This usually wins for one traveller. The continuous car usually wins for **2+ people** or when nature flexibility matters more than the one-way surcharge.

## Lithuania → Poland
Vilnius→Warsaw overland is the ugly transfer of the route: Rome2Rio currently shows roughly **6 h 27 min bus+train** or **8 h 15 min train**. Keep it as one deliberate transfer day. Once in Poland, Warsaw→Kraków fast train is about **2 h 18 min**.

**Do not keep the Baltic rental into Poland by default.** Sixt Baltic terms show Poland can trigger a foreign-use zone fee (~€150), and one-way drop to Warsaw can be far more expensive (Europcar Latvia example €665.50).

## Bottlenecks
1. One-way Baltic car fee.
2. Vilnius→Warsaw long overland leg.
3. Car-camping/hammock plans must not be conflated with legal parking: a legal hammock site does not automatically mean the rental car may be parked beside it.
4. National-park camping restrictions supersede general roaming rights.
