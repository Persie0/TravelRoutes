## Where the hammock is actually used
The itinerary intentionally avoids claiming a random spot *inside* every national park. In Finland, camp in ordinary legal forest under Everyman's Rights or at park-designated camping areas depending each park's rules. In Estonia use RMK sites where possible. Latvia is flexible in state forest but protected-area rules still win. Lithuania uses official campgrounds. Poland uses `Zanocuj w lesie` zones if you add a forest night.

## 30–32 day upgrade
Add: (1) a second Oulanka/Karhunkierros day, (2) an extra Gauja/Cēsis day or Saaremaa detour, (3) one extra Kraków/Ojców/Tatras day. This version is noticeably less transfer-heavy.
