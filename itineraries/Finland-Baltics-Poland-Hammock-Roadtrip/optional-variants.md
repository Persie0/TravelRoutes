# Worthwhile Variants

## 1. Nature-max (recommended if Poland is optional)
Cut Warsaw entirely and give the recovered 1–2 days to **Oulanka + Inari/Lemmenjoki** or add **Saaremaa** in Estonia. Fly home from Vilnius. This is the best pure-nature version.

## 2. Ruska + Aurora (September)
Keep the same route but start in Lapland around the first/second week of September. Bring a genuine cold-weather underquilt + top quilt/sleeping bag. Spend an extra night in Saariselkä or Inari to wait for clear skies.

## 3. Midnight Sun (July)
Start in early July, retain more late-night hikes, and shift some city sightseeing into daytime. Bring aggressive mosquito protection. Expect busier Oulanka trailheads.

## 4. Budget solo
- no Baltic one-way car;
- Tallinn→Riga→Vilnius by Lux Express/FlixBus/train;
- one short Lahemaa rental or bus day;
- Gauja via Sigulda rail/bus;
- skip Kuldīga + Curonian Spit if transport becomes too fragmented.

## 5. Car-heavy from Oulu
Fly to Oulu only if airfare/rental economics are clearly better. Oulu→Rovaniemi is not bad driving, but the scenery-value-per-hour is weaker than the Rovaniemi loop. Add Hailuoto if you specifically want coast + hammock.

## 6. 30–32 day relaxed
Add one Oulanka day, one Gauja/Curonian weather buffer, and one Kraków day. This is the most balanced version for a true month.
