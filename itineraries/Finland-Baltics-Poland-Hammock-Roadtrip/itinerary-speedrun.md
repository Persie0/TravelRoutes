# Speedrun itinerary — Finland → Baltics → Poland

## Goal

Keep the important S/A-tier nature and city experiences, but remove duplicate recovery days, slow beach time and unnecessary overnight stops.

- Full route: 28 days
- 60:40: ~14 days, large regions cut
- **Speedrun theoretical minimum: ~18 days**
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum: 20 days**

Two extra days are useful because Lapland hikes, Baltic road legs and Curonian Spit weather are not metro-style connections.

## What remains protected

Helsinki/Suomenlinna, Oulanka, Riisitunturi, Pyhä-Luosto, UKK/Saariselkä, Inari/SIIDA, Tallinn, Lahemaa, Riga, Gauja, Kuldīga, Curonian Spit, Trakai/Vilnius, Warsaw and Kraków.

## Speedrun rules

1. Use both Finnish sleeper trains; do not burn daylight driving the full north-south axis.
2. Camp/hammock only where legal and practical; a Speedrun does not justify breaking protected-area rules.
3. Treat Pärnu and Kaunas as high-value through-stops rather than hotel moves.
4. Keep one of Days 19–20 flexible if weather ruins a signature Lapland hike or Curonian dunes day.
5. Do not compress hikes by starting them too late after long drives.

**Speedrun removes slow mornings and duplicate nights, not the signature northern nature chain.**
