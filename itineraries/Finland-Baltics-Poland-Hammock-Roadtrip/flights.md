# Flight Strategy

Research snapshot: **24 August 2026**. User is flexible between July, August and September of the next summer; only part of August 2027 was inside the connected fare tool's current horizon.

## Best shell
**Open jaw:** Austria → **Helsinki (HEL)**, then overland all the way south, **Kraków (KRK) → Austria**.

This is structurally better than returning to Finland, because the whole itinerary is one southbound chain after Lapland.

## Current indicative Skyscanner datapoints
- **VIE → HEL:** direct Finnair examples around **€125 one-way** on 5 and 10 August 2027; a Lufthansa connection appeared from €156 on a later date.
- **VIE → OUL:** no indicative quote returned in the tested August 2027 search; do not infer that Oulu is expensive, only that no result was available in this snapshot.
- **KRK → VIE:** an August 2027 market check returned about **€82 one-way with a connection (LOT)** or **€121 direct (Austrian)** on the sampled date.

These are **not a matched 28-day booking pair**; they are route-price signals only. Re-run live pricing when the exact return date enters the one-year search horizon.

## Airports to test before booking
1. VIE→HEL / KRK→VIE — default.
2. BUD→HEL / KRK→VIE — worth checking if Vienna outbound spikes.
3. VIE/BUD→OUL — only if the fare saving outweighs the extra rail logistics.
4. VIE→RVN — can be excellent if it avoids Helsinki on a shorter trip, but the full 28-day itinerary benefits from seeing Helsinki.

## Raw cheapest vs best value
A €30–50 cheaper flight to Oulu or a connecting itinerary can lose its advantage after airport transfer, luggage, an extra night or a wasted half-day. Prefer the open-jaw structure unless the alternative saves **materially** more than ~€100 after all positioning costs.

Skyscanner attribution: fare data is indicative and changes continuously; verify on Skyscanner/live airline inventory before purchase.
