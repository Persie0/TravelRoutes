# Worked Example — August 2027

**Illustrative period:** about four weeks centred on August 2027. Exact return pricing is not yet fully inside the connected one-year fare horizon as of 24 August 2026, so this file is a booking layer rather than a fake exact quote.

## Booking order
1. Lock a good **VIE/BUD → HEL** one-way and **KRK → VIE/BUD** return shell once both dates are searchable.
2. Reserve the Helsinki→Rovaniemi sleeper soon after train inventory opens; couchette/cabin converts transport into accommodation.
3. Reserve Rovaniemi rental for the 8–9 day loop and confirm sleeping gear may remain in vehicle / no restrictions relevant to your use.
4. Reserve Helsinki→Tallinn ferry.
5. Quote Baltic one-way car Tallinn→Vilnius **before** building the middle section around it. If surcharge is high, switch to the bus/local-rental variant.
6. Reserve Curonian Spit official overnight site if staying in/near the protected area.
7. Buy Vilnius→Warsaw and Warsaw→Kraków rail/bus when schedules open.

## Fare snapshot available now
Skyscanner indicative August 2027 data found VIE→HEL direct around **€125** on sampled early-August dates. A separate KRK→VIE sample showed **€82 connecting / €121 direct** on one August date. Those samples prove the open-jaw shell can be competitive, but they do not yet form a matched 28-day itinerary.

## Gear check for this period
August is the default hammock month: tarp, integrated bug net, underquilt/insulated mat, and a +5°C-comfort sleep system. If your actual trip runs into September, upgrade warmth rather than gambling on a summer bag.
