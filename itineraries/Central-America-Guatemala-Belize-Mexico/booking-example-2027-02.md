This is the date-specific booking layer. The evergreen route remains in `general-28-day-route.md`.

Detailed day-by-day schedule: **[`itinerary.md`](./itinerary.md)**.

## Reference dates

**1–28 February 2027**

February is the best all-round month in the route climate model: strong Guatemala highland visibility odds, Belize dry-season conditions, pleasant Yucatán walking temperatures and no normal Atlantic hurricane-season exposure.

## Indicative long-haul fare shells observed 2026-08-23

Skyscanner indicative data; these are not locked fares.

| Shell | Indicative airfare | Positioning implication |
|---|---:|---|
| BUD→GUA Feb 1 + CUN→BUD Feb 28 | **~€811** | raw fare winner; requires Budapest at both ends |
| VIE→GUA Feb 1 + CUN→BUD Feb 28 | **~€830** | only ~€19 more airfare; avoids outbound Vienna→Budapest positioning |
| VIE→GUA Feb 1 + CUN→VIE Feb 26 | **~€872** | simpler airport logistics but two fewer days |
| CUN→VIE Feb 24 alone | ~€367 | useful for a shorter 24-day version |
| CUN→BUD Feb 28 alone | ~€358 | strongest late-Feb return observed |

### Best raw fare

**BUD→GUA + CUN→BUD ≈ €811**

### Best practical shell from Vienna

**VIE→GUA + CUN→BUD ≈ €830**, because a €19 fare premium can be cheaper than positioning to Budapest twice.

Always compare:
1. VIE→GUA + CUN→VIE
2. BUD→GUA + CUN→BUD
3. VIE→GUA + CUN→BUD
4. BUD→GUA + CUN→VIE

and price the train/bus, checked luggage, food and buffer time to/from Budapest.
