# Speedrun itinerary — Guatemala → Belize → Yucatán

## Goal

This is **not** the same thing as the 60:40 itinerary.

- **60:40:** deliberately removes lower-value or high-detour destinations to preserve roughly the best 60% of experience value in ~40% of the time.
- **Speedrun:** keeps essentially **all S-tier and A-tier experiences** from the full route, but compresses each base to the **minimum practical stay needed to see the important sights**.

The Speedrun sacrifices comfort, redundancy, slow mornings and weather buffers before it sacrifices major sights.

## Result

- **Theoretical minimum:** about **16 days in-region** if international flight times cooperate, transport runs cleanly, Acatenango weather is usable and the Blue Hole is done as a scenic flight rather than a full-day dive trip.
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum:** **17 days in-region**. The extra day should be held as a floating weather/transport buffer and inserted where conditions demand it.
- **If you want a full-day Great Blue Hole / Lighthouse Reef dive trip:** add **1 day**.
- **If your arrival in Guatemala is late afternoon/evening:** add **0.5–1 day** for Antigua rather than pretending the city can be seen after dark.

This is an intentionally intense itinerary. It is designed for a traveler who would rather see another major sight than spend an extra afternoon relaxing.

## Minimum viable time by base

| Base / area | Minimum useful time | Important experiences protected | Compression logic |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Antigua | 0.5–1 sightseeing day + Acatenango 2D/1N | Antigua historic centre; Acatenango/Fuego | Sightsee immediately after arrival; start hike next morning |
| Lake Atitlán | 1 full day | lake scenery; San Juan; one additional village/viewpoint | early first boat, cluster villages, no idle resort day |
| Semuc Champey / Lanquín | 1 full activity day | mirador + pools/caves depending preference | accept transfer days but stay only as long as the core activity requires |
| Flores / Tikal | 1 full sightseeing day | Tikal; short Flores waterfront only as arrival evening | skip a separate Flores sightseeing day |
| San Ignacio | 2 activity days | Xunantunich; ATM Cave | do Xunantunich on border-arrival afternoon, ATM next full day |
| Caye Caulker | 1–1.5 activity days | island atmosphere; Hol Chan + Shark Ray Alley; Blue Hole visual experience | half-day reef tour + scenic Blue Hole flight if schedules align |
| Bacalar | 0.5–1 day | Lagoon of Seven Colors | sunset on arrival + sunrise/morning boat/kayak next day |

# 16-day theoretical-minimum schedule

## Day 1 — Arrive Guatemala → Antigua

**Target:** land early enough to reach Antigua around midday.

- Transfer GUA → Antigua immediately.
- Drop bags; do not wait for normal hotel check-in if luggage storage is available.
- Compact Antigua circuit: Parque Central, Cathedral area, Arco de Santa Catalina, Iglesia de La Merced, key ruined church/convent exterior/interior according to opening hours, Cerro de la Cruz near sunset if time allows.
- Early night for Acatenango.

**Sleep:** Antigua  
**Intensity:** 🔴  
**Failure condition:** late international arrival; if so, use the floating Day 17 buffer here.

## Day 2 — Acatenango / Fuego ascent

- Early tour departure from Antigua.
- Climb to base camp.
- Sunset, Fuego views and night eruptions if volcanic/weather conditions cooperate.

**Sleep:** Acatenango camp  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 3 — Summit → Antigua → Lake Atitlán

- Pre-dawn summit attempt if included/conditions allow.
- Descend to Antigua, normally around late morning/noon depending operator.
- Eat, collect stored luggage and transfer directly to Panajachel / Lake Atitlán.
- Sunset/evening by the lake.

**Sleep:** Lake Atitlán  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 4 — Lake Atitlán speedrun

- Take the first practical public boat.
- Prioritize **San Juan La Laguna** for murals, weaving/cooperative culture and village atmosphere.
- Add one geographically efficient second stop such as San Pedro or another village/viewpoint matching interests.
- Return before boat frequency drops in the evening.
- Pack for the long Lanquín/Semuc transfer.

**Sleep:** Lake Atitlán  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 5 — Lake Atitlán → Lanquín / Semuc

- Earliest practical shuttle/transfer toward Lanquín.
- Treat this as a transport day rather than trying to force a fake half-day attraction into an already long road journey.
- Check in, dinner, sleep.

**Sleep:** Lanquín  
**Intensity:** 🟡/🔴 due to transfer length

## Day 6 — Semuc Champey

- Start early.
- Mirador first while temperatures are lower.
- Pools/swimming afterward.
- Add cave/river experience only if it fits safely without rushing the core site.
- Return to Lanquín and prepare for Flores transfer.

**Sleep:** Lanquín  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 7 — Lanquín → Flores

- Earliest practical transfer to Flores.
- Use arrival evening for the **Flores island/waterfront** rather than allocating a separate sightseeing day.
- Arrange Tikal transport/ticket details before bed.

**Sleep:** Flores  
**Intensity:** 🟡/🔴

## Day 8 — Tikal

- Very early departure.
- Full high-value Tikal circuit: main plazas/temples, major complexes and jungle trails according to current access rules.
- Return to Flores.
- Do **not** add Yaxhá in the Speedrun unless another day is available; it is excellent but not required to preserve the route's S/A core.

**Sleep:** Flores  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 9 — Flores → San Ignacio + Xunantunich

- Cross Guatemala → Belize as early as practical.
- Continue to San Ignacio, store bags/check in.
- Use the afternoon for **Xunantunich** if border/transport timing and opening hours allow.
- If delays make that impossible, move Xunantunich to the floating buffer day rather than cutting ATM.

**Sleep:** San Ignacio  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 10 — ATM Cave

- Full-day **Actun Tunichil Muknal** guided experience.
- No second major attraction is assumed after ATM; it is already a demanding day.

**Sleep:** San Ignacio  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 11 — San Ignacio → Belize City → Caye Caulker

- Leave early for Belize City.
- Connect to the next sensible ferry rather than overnighting Belize City.
- Caye Caulker afternoon/evening: walk/cycle the island, Split/waterfront, sunset.

**Sleep:** Caye Caulker  
**Intensity:** 🟡

## Day 12 — Belize reef speedrun

**Fastest all-important version:**

- Half-day **Hol Chan Marine Reserve + Shark Ray Alley** snorkeling.
- Combine with a **Great Blue Hole scenic flight** if a same-day schedule is available and weather permits.

This preserves both the route's best normal snorkeling experience and the visual/iconic Blue Hole experience without consuming a second full boat day.

**For divers:** do not compress Lighthouse Reef into this day. Add +1 day for the proper full-day dive excursion.

**Sleep:** Caye Caulker  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 13 — Caye Caulker → Belize City → Bacalar

- First useful ferry to Belize City.
- Continue north across the Belize–Mexico border to Bacalar.
- Use late afternoon/evening for lagoon viewpoints, waterfront and sunset.

**Sleep:** Bacalar  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 14 — Bacalar morning → Valladolid

- Sunrise or early morning lagoon time.
- Short boat/kayak session if it starts early enough.
- Leave around late morning/noon for Valladolid.
- Evening Valladolid circuit: central plaza, cathedral/exteriors, streets, food, optional nearby town cenote if opening time remains.

**Sleep:** Valladolid  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 15 — Chichén Itzá → cenote → Cancún

- Leave Valladolid before the main tour-bus wave.
- Enter **Chichén Itzá** at or near opening.
- Cover the major monumental core efficiently rather than lingering through peak heat.
- Add a nearby cenote after the ruins.
- Return/continue east and transfer to Cancún in the afternoon/evening.

**Sleep:** Cancún  
**Intensity:** 🔴

## Day 16 — Cancún / fly home

- If flight is late: short beach/swim/breakfast block.
- Airport with a conservative international buffer.

**Intensity:** 🟢/🟡

# Day 17 — recommended floating Speedrun buffer

The **recommended** Speedrun is 17 days, not 16, because several core experiences are weather/schedule dependent.

Do not pre-assign this day unless booking constraints require it. Place it where it protects the most value:

1. **Acatenango/Fuego weather** — highest priority use.
2. **Belize reef / Blue Hole weather** — second priority.
3. **Late international arrival / Antigua**.
4. **Guatemala–Belize or Belize–Mexico transport/border disruption**.
5. **Xunantunich** if Day 9 border timing makes the afternoon visit impossible.
6. **Recovery** only if everything runs perfectly.

# Speedrun rules used

1. **Never cut an S-tier sight merely to make the trip shorter.**
2. Keep A-tier sights unless they are impossible, seasonal, or highly interest-specific.
3. Add B-tier sights only when marginal time cost is near zero.
4. Move between bases after the day's core attraction whenever practical.
5. Use early opening hours before crowds and heat.
6. Use arrival evenings for compact historic centres/waterfronts rather than assigning another full day.
7. Store luggage instead of waiting for hotel check-in.
8. Cluster sights geographically; do not criss-cross a city for a checklist.
9. Pay for a faster shuttle/flight/transfer when it saves a meaningful fraction of a sightseeing day.
10. Do not schedule impossible connections: border hours, ferry departures, attraction closing times and check-in logistics still matter.
11. Weather-dependent S-tier experiences get priority over fixed urban sightseeing.
12. The schedule should feel **aggressive but physically possible**, not like a spreadsheet that ignores meals, luggage and travel friction.

# Which version should you choose?

| Version | Approx. in-region time | Main optimization target | What gets sacrificed |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Full | ~28 days | best overall experience | very little |
| Speedrun | **16 theoretical / 17 recommended** | see essentially all important S/A sights in minimum practical time | comfort, spare afternoons, redundancy, most B/C sights |
| 60:40 | ~12 days | highest experience value per day | entire A/B destinations when their detour cost is too high |

For a traveler who explicitly wants to **see almost everything important but move fast**, the **17-day Speedrun** is the strongest default. For a traveler who only has ~12 days, switch to the 60:40 route instead of pretending the 17-day route can be squeezed further without meaningful losses.
