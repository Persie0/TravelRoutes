# Flight strategy

Research snapshot: 2026-08-23.

## Long-haul rule

Do not force the trip to start and end at the same airport. Search four open-jaw shells:

1. VIE→GUA + CUN→VIE
2. BUD→GUA + CUN→BUD
3. VIE→GUA + CUN→BUD
4. BUD→GUA + CUN→VIE

Then compare **true door-to-door cost**, not airfare alone.

## February 2027 indicative anchors

- BUD→GUA Feb 1: ~€453
- VIE→GUA Feb 1: ~€472
- CUN→BUD Feb 28: ~€358
- CUN→VIE Feb 26: ~€400
- CUN→VIE Feb 24: ~€367

Raw-fare winner for using essentially the whole month:
**BUD→GUA Feb 1 + CUN→BUD Feb 28 ≈ €811**.

Practical mixed-airport option from Vienna:
**VIE→GUA Feb 1 + CUN→BUD Feb 28 ≈ €830**.

The ~€19 airfare difference is small enough that avoiding one Vienna↔Budapest positioning leg can make the mixed shell superior overall.

## Strategic regional flights

### GUA → FRS

For the 60:40 itinerary, this is the most valuable internal flight.

Current schedule research shows:
- nonstop;
- Avianca + TAG Airlines;
- roughly 50–60 minutes;
- multiple daily frequencies in current schedules.

This converts a major overland transfer into a short flight.

### BZE → CUN

Current Tropic Air service:
- nonstop;
- roughly 1h30;
- daily service shown in current route/schedule data.

Rome2Rio's Caye Caulker→Cancún comparison currently shows:
- fly: ~4h43 total;
- ferry+train: ~9h21;
- ferry+bus: ~11h35.

This flight is generally **not the budget choice**; it is the 60:40 time-saving choice.

## Mexico City strategy

CUN→MEX/NLU domestic fares can be very cheap on selected dates. Add Mexico City only when:
- you have +2–4 days; or
- MEX→Europe materially improves the return fare.

Do not fly to Mexico City merely to save €20 if the domestic flight, airport transfer and extra hotel eliminate the saving.
