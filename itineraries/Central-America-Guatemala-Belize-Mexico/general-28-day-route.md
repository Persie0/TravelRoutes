# General ~28-day route: Guatemala → Belize → Yucatán

This is the **date-agnostic master itinerary**. Use it whenever the trip falls in a suitable season; the separate February 2027 file is only a worked airfare/date example.

## Master route

**Europe → Guatemala City → Antigua → Acatenango/Fuego → Lake Atitlán → Semuc Champey → Flores → Tikal (+ Yaxhá) → San Ignacio → Caye Caulker → Bacalar → Valladolid → Chichén Itzá (+ Ek Balam) → Cancún → Europe**

Optional extensions/substitutions:

- **Mexico City:** add 2–4 days at the end via CUN→MEX/NLU.
- **El Paredón:** add 2 nights from Antigua if surfing matters.
- **Caracol + Mountain Pine Ridge:** use a third San Ignacio day if Maya archaeology is a major interest.
- **Extra reef day:** keep one flexible Caye Caulker day because wind/sea state can cancel boat trips even in good season.

## Why this route order is efficient

1. **It is geographically linear.** Guatemala highlands → northern Petén → western Belize → Belize coast → southern Yucatán → Cancún.
2. **No Lake Atitlán → Guatemala City backtrack.** Go directly toward Lanquín/Semuc Champey.
3. **Belize City is transit, not a forced stop.** San Ignacio → Belize City → Caye Caulker can normally be done in one travel day.
4. **Bacalar solves the worst transfer.** Rome2Rio's Caye Caulker→Valladolid routing is around 10 hours; breaking at Bacalar turns it into two reasonable legs while adding a very worthwhile lagoon destination.
5. **Valladolid is the correct Chichén Itzá base.** Mexico's tourism ministry notes Chichén Itzá is about 45 km from Valladolid; Valladolid also sits close to Ek Balam and has its own historic/cenote attractions.
6. **Cancún is a convenient endpoint.** Keep it short unless you specifically want resort/beach time.

# Recommended 28-day allocation

## Days 1–5 — Antigua + Acatenango/Fuego

### Day 1 — Arrive Guatemala City → Antigua

Transfer to Antigua unless the flight arrives so late that an airport night is more sensible.

### Day 2 — Antigua

Prioritize:

- Parque Central / Cathedral area;
- Santa Catalina Arch;
- La Merced;
- Las Capuchinas / Santa Clara or another major ruin complex;
- Cerro de la Cruz for the city/Volcán Agua view.

UNESCO recognizes Antigua for its preserved Renaissance-influenced grid and its major Baroque monuments/earthquake ruins.

### Days 3–4 — Acatenango overnight

- 2D/1N guided climb.
- High camp facing Fuego if the chosen operator/camp permits.
- Sunset/night volcanic views when cloud/activity cooperate.
- Optional Fuego saddle extension only if fitness + guide + conditions support it.
- Acatenango summit around sunrise.
- Return to Antigua on Day 4.

### Day 5 — Antigua flex/recovery

This is not wasted time: it protects the trip from bad volcano weather, gives your legs a break and keeps the next long route segment from becoming painful.

**Cut first in a shorter trip** if Acatenango runs exactly on schedule and you recover quickly.

---

# Days 6–8 — Lake Atitlán

### Day 6 — Antigua → Lake Atitlán

Travel to Panajachel, then continue by boat to your preferred village.

**Best bases:**

- San Juan La Laguna — culture/art/co-ops.
- Santa Cruz — scenery/quiet.
- Panajachel — easiest transport/logistics.

### Day 7 — San Juan + second village

Good pairing: San Juan + Santiago Atitlán.

### Day 8 — second full lake day

Choose one rather than rushing all villages:

- Santa Catarina Palopó;
- Santiago Atitlán if not done Day 7;
- kayaking / lake time;
- safe locally recommended hike/viewpoint;
- slower recovery day.

**Why 3 nights:** Lake Atitlán is a destination, not merely a road stop.

---

# Days 9–11 — Semuc Champey

### Day 9 — Lake Atitlán → Lanquín/Semuc

Long transfer. **Do not route back through Guatemala City unless current transport availability actually requires it.**

### Day 10 — Semuc Champey

Protect these:

1. Mirador hike.
2. Limestone pools/swimming.

Optional cave/tubing activities come after those, not instead of them.

### Day 11 — Semuc → Flores

Rome2Rio planning estimate: bus about **7 h 20 min**, shuttle about **8 h**.

---

# Days 12–14 — Petén: Tikal + Yaxhá/Flores

### Day 12 — Tikal

Full day. Tikal is one of the major Maya civilization sites and a UNESCO mixed cultural/natural World Heritage property.

Do not rush only the headline temples: the forest setting and wildlife are part of the experience.

### Day 13 — Yaxhá sunset

Best second Petén ruin for this route because the lake/forest/sunset atmosphere differs from Tikal.

If you are already archaeologically saturated, turn this into a Flores/Lake Petén Itzá rest day.

### Day 14 — Flores → San Ignacio, Belize

Rome2Rio route overview: bus/shuttle about **2 h 30 min** before allowing for border formalities.

Do not schedule a non-refundable afternoon activity immediately after the border.

---

# Days 15–17 — San Ignacio / Cayo

### Day 15 — ATM Cave

**Highest priority in western Belize.** Official Belize tourism describes a drive and jungle hike with stream crossings, a swim into the cave and a physically demanding guided archaeological cave experience.

### Day 16 — Xunantunich + Cahal Pech

Xunantunich gets priority. Official Belize tourism highlights the hand-cranked ferry, six plazas, 25+ temples/palaces and the ~130-ft El Castillo.

Cahal Pech works well as a compact second site.

### Day 17 — choose your Cayo extra

**A — Caracol + Mountain Pine Ridge:** best for archaeology/nature maximization.  
**B — Barton Creek / nature:** adds variety after multiple archaeological sites.  
**C — rest/local day:** sensible if ATM was exhausting.

For a first 28-day trip, Caracol is optional rather than mandatory because Tikal, Yaxhá, Xunantunich, Chichén and Ek Balam are already included.

---

# Days 18–21 — Caye Caulker + Belize Barrier Reef

### Day 18 — San Ignacio → Belize City → Caye Caulker

Rome2Rio overview:

- San Ignacio→Belize City: roughly 2–2.5 h by bus/shuttle.
- Belize City→Caye Caulker: ferry roughly 45 min.

No default Belize City hotel night.

### Day 19 — Hol Chan + Shark Ray Alley

**Best snorkel day for most travelers.** Belize tourism describes Hol Chan as the country's first marine reserve, with reef, seagrass, mangrove and Shark Ray Alley zones.

### Day 20 — Great Blue Hole / Lighthouse Reef or alternative

- **Qualified diver:** Lighthouse Reef/Blue Hole full day is a major option.
- **Non-diver:** consider a scenic flight or another reef-focused snorkel day instead; the Blue Hole's geometry is especially striking from above.

### Day 21 — weather buffer / island day

Keep this flexible. Wind/sea state can move or cancel marine excursions even during dry season.

If everything ran perfectly, use it for Caye Caulker itself, kayaking, swimming or another reef trip.

---

# Days 22–23 — Bacalar

### Day 22 — Caye Caulker → Bacalar

Rome2Rio's fastest route overview was about **5 h 12 min**, with other combinations longer. Border/boat connection slack can make the real day longer.

This is still preferable to pushing all the way to Valladolid in one ~10-hour chain.

### Day 23 — Bacalar Lagoon

Mexico's tourism ministry highlights:

- Laguna de Bacalar / Lagoon of Seven Colors;
- Cenote Azul;
- Fuerte de San Felipe;
- Museo de la Piratería;
- central plaza.

Prioritize low-impact lagoon time + Fort San Felipe.

---

# Days 24–27 — Valladolid + Chichén Itzá + Ek Balam

### Day 24 — Bacalar → Valladolid

Rome2Rio bus planning estimate: roughly **4 h 45 min**.

Evening walk:

- Zócalo;
- San Servacio;
- Calzada de los Frailes;
- Yucatecan food.

### Day 25 — Chichén Itzá + cenote

Stay in Valladolid and arrive close to opening time. The INAH site confirms Chichén Itzá's World Heritage status and extensive monumental complex.

Priorities:

- El Castillo/Kukulcán;
- Great Ball Court;
- Temple of the Warriors;
- Sacred Cenote;
- observatory/additional groups if time permits.

Add a cenote after the site.

### Day 26 — Ek Balam + Valladolid

INAH describes Ek Balam's walls, murals, stelae, plazas, palaces and richly decorated Acropolis.

Use the remaining day for Valladolid's colonial sights / Cenote Zací if access is suitable.

### Day 27 — Valladolid → Cancún

Rome2Rio overview:

- ADO bus ~2 h 40 min;
- Tren Maya ~2 h 56 min;
- drive ~1 h 46 min.

Use Cancún as both a beach afternoon and an international-flight buffer.

---

# Day 28 — Cancún → Europe

Fly home.

This preserves a full month-like route without requiring an internal flight.

# Optional Mexico City version

Mexico City is excellent, but it should be added by **reallocating days**, not by making the core route more rushed.

## 28 days including Mexico City

Cut/reallocate:

- Day 5 Antigua recovery → cut if volcano schedule is stable.
- Day 13 Yaxhá → optional cut.
- Day 17 Caracol/Cayo extra → cut.
- Day 21 Caye Caulker buffer → retain only if forecast requires it.

Then:

- reach Cancún around Day 25;
- fly CUN→MEX/NLU;
- Days 26–27 Mexico City;
- Day 28 fly Europe.

### Mexico City priorities for only 2 days

**Day A:** Centro Histórico, Zócalo, Metropolitan Cathedral exterior/interior as open, Templo Mayor, Palacio de Bellas Artes area.  
**Day B:** National Museum of Anthropology + Chapultepec.  

Add Teotihuacán only if you can give Mexico City a third full day.

---

# Optional surfing version: El Paredón

Cancún is not the strongest surf justification for this route. If surfing matters, add **El Paredón, Guatemala** after Antigua.

Recommended change:

- Days 5–6: Antigua→El Paredón, surf/beach.
- Day 7: return toward Antigua/Atitlán.
- Remove Yaxhá + Caracol or one lake day to preserve a 28-day total.

This is a geographic detour south, so only make it if surf is a meaningful trip goal.

---

# Best cut order for 24–26 days

Cut in this order:

1. Caracol/Mountain Pine Ridge extra day.
2. Ek Balam.
3. Yaxhá.
4. Antigua recovery day if Acatenango timing is secure.
5. One Lake Atitlán day.
6. Bacalar's full day — but still consider sleeping there to break the Belize→Valladolid transfer.

Protect:

- Acatenango/Fuego;
- Tikal;
- ATM Cave;
- Hol Chan/Shark Ray Alley;
- Chichén Itzá;
- at least one full Lake Atitlán day;
- Semuc Champey.

---

# Best expansion for 30–32 days

Add in this order:

1. Mexico City 3 days.
2. Keep Yaxhá and Caracol.
3. Extra Caye Caulker reef/weather day.
4. Extra Lake Atitlán day.
5. El Paredón if surfing matters.
