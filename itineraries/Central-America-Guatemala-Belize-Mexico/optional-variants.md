# Optional route variants

The core 28-day route is the best general first-trip balance. These variants change the allocation only when a specific priority justifies it.

## 1. Relaxed ~28 days

Cut:
- Yaxhá
- Caracol
- Ek Balam

Reallocate:
- +1 Lake Atitlán slow day
- +1 Caye Caulker weather/rest day
- +1 Bacalar/Valladolid slow day

Best for: lower travel fatigue, couples, people who do not want consecutive red-intensity days.

## 2. Maximum sightseeing, 30–32 days

Keep:
- Yaxhá
- Caracol + Mountain Pine Ridge
- Ek Balam
- Great Blue Hole/Lighthouse Reef

Add:
- Mexico City 3 days

Best for: travelers who recover quickly and prefer breadth over downtime.

## 3. Budget version

Keep the same geographic order but:
- use public bus/shuttle combinations over private transfers;
- keep Belize City as same-day transit;
- use ferry rather than domestic flights;
- skip scenic Blue Hole flight;
- prioritize Hol Chan over premium reef trips;
- skip Mexico City unless the long-haul fare itself becomes cheaper from MEX.

Do **not** cut Bacalar automatically: in the full overland route it turns a ~10-hour Caye Caulker→Valladolid chain into two manageable days.

## 4. Hiking / nature-heavy version

Add:
- extra Lake Atitlán day;
- Mountain Pine Ridge;
- extra reef day;
- optional El Paredón only if surfing is genuinely a priority.

Cut:
- Yaxhá;
- Ek Balam;
- one generic Cancún day.

## 5. Archaeology-heavy version

Keep/add:
- Tikal
- Yaxhá
- Xunantunich
- Caracol
- Chichén Itzá
- Ek Balam
- optionally Cahal Pech

Cut:
- one Lake Atitlán day;
- Caye weather buffer only when marine forecast is stable;
- Bacalar full day if needed, while still using Bacalar as an overnight split.

## 6. Reef / beach-heavy version

Keep:
- Hol Chan + Shark Ray Alley
- Lighthouse Reef / Blue Hole if diving
- at least one flexible Caye day

Add:
- another reef excursion or island day.

Cut:
- Yaxhá
- Caracol
- Ek Balam

Cancún is useful for beach time but should not displace Belize reef days.

## 7. Mexico City ending

Best when:
- trip length is 30–32 days; or
- CUN→MEX + MEX→Europe is close enough in price to CUN→Europe that Mexico City is effectively a cheap add-on.

For 3 days:
1. Centro Histórico + Templo Mayor + Bellas Artes area
2. Anthropology Museum + Chapultepec
3. Teotihuacán

For a strict 28-day trip, pay for Mexico City by cutting Yaxhá, Caracol and/or Ek Balam rather than compressing every stop.

## 8. Surfing correction: El Paredón

If surfing matters, the Pacific coast at **El Paredón, Guatemala** is a more logical surf-focused detour than adding days in Cancún.

Add 2 nights after Antigua and remove:
- Yaxhá and/or
- Caracol and/or
- Ek Balam

This is geographically a detour south, so it should be intentional.

## 9. 60:40 / time-is-money version

Use:
- GUA→FRS nonstop flight;
- BZE→CUN nonstop flight.

Cut:
- Lake Atitlán by default;
- Semuc;
- Bacalar;
- secondary archaeology.

See [`itinerary-60-40.md`](./itinerary-60-40.md).
