# 28-day optimized South Caucasus itinerary (evergreen)

**Direction:** Baku (Azerbaijan) → Sheki → outbound-only Azerbaijan/Georgia crossing → Kakheti → Tbilisi → Kazbegi → Kutaisi → Svaneti 4-day trek → Vardzia → Gyumri/Lori → Dilijan → Yerevan/Tatev. The trip starts by **flying into Azerbaijan**. Azerbaijan–Armenia has no open tourist crossing.

This is an ambitious nature-and-culture itinerary, not a relaxed vacation. For a more forgiving September trip, book **30 days** and put one extra day before the four-day Svaneti trek and one before the return flight. Daily times are planning estimates, not confirmed operator schedules. Major Baku–Georgia land exit and Bavra transfer MUST be rechecked before committing non-refundable bookings.

## Critical trek and border assumptions

- The Mestia → Zhabeshi → Adishi → Iprari → Ushguli trek is a **4-day walking route**; overnight village guesthouses must be open. The Adishchala river crossing can be dangerous: take a horse/guide if necessary and **never attempt a hazardous ford**. Confirm route conditions, offline GPS and the return 4×4 before setting out. [Visit Svaneti](https://visit-svaneti.ge/en/treks/mestia-to-ushguli) · [Transcaucasian Trail stage](https://transcaucasiantrail.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Adishi-to-Ushguli.pdf).
- Azerbaijan **land entry** remains restricted in the November 2026 source snapshot; Austrian visitors who **flew in may in principle exit overland to Georgia**. The specific Balakan/Mazımçay/Lagodekhi checkpoint and same-day onward transfer must be verified for September 2027. Flight fallback: return Sheki → Baku, then fly Baku → Tbilisi; budget 1–2 lost days. [Austrian BMEIA](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/aserbaidschan) · [Azerbaijan MFA](https://mfa.gov.az/en/category/entry-rules-to-the-republic-of-azerbaijan-during-covid-19-pandemic).
- Avoid border-adjacent conflict areas, including restricted sections of the Ijevan–Noyemberyan M16/H26 route. This itinerary uses a **Gyumri → Vanadzor → Lori → Dilijan** approach rather than that corridor. Reassess Tatev/Syunik travel advice before departure. [UK FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/armenia/regional-risks) · [BMEIA Armenia](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/armenien).
- The **Vardzia → Bavra → Gyumri** transfer is rural with scarce through-public transport: arrange two local drivers and budget a long border day; do not assume a cross-border rental car is permitted.

## What to skip if exhausted

Drop Lahij (save 1 night), the Gyumri/Haghpat detour (save 1 day), or shorten city time first. Do **not** squeeze the four-day trek into 2 days without changing the activity to a 4×4 Ushguli visit.

## Offline essentials

Offline map/GPX; layered waterproof kit; cash for mountain villages; travel insurance including remote hiking and evacuation; offline e-visa and passport scans; booked mountain guesthouses; backup day and driver phone number. Flights, crossings, geopolitics and prices are **not guarantees for 2027**.
