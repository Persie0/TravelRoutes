# Transport and route optimization

## The decisive border rule (snapshot: 8 November 2026)

- **Azerbaijan entry:** ordinary foreign tourists generally enter **by air**. Austria requires Azerbaijan e-visa; the **Azerbaijan–Armenia crossing is not available**. Azerbaijan's MFA says foreign visitors can **exit** Azerbaijan overland without special permission; Austrian BMEIA separately confirms land exit in principle. This is one-way asymmetric, not an open two-way road corridor. [BMEIA Azerbaijan](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/aserbaidschan) · [Azerbaijan MFA](https://mfa.gov.az/en/category/entry-rules-to-the-republic-of-azerbaijan-during-covid-19-pandemic).
- **Preferred outbound crossing:** **Balakan/Mazımçay → Lagodekhi** from Sheki, followed by Sighnaghi/Kakheti. It is a *proposed planning crossing*, not a confirmed ticket/reservation or guarantee for September 2027. [Lagodekhi checkpoint coordinates](https://mapcarta.com/N2325407146) · [historical crossing list](https://onstravels.az/border-crossings). Check tourist pedestrian/cab access in both directions of exit **again** with border authorities and a local driver shortly before travel. Some online guides conflate *entry* and *exit* prohibitions.
- **Georgia→Armenia:** open land crossings including Sadakhlo/Bagratashen and Ninotsminda/Bavra (as of 2026). Our route chooses **Ninotsminda/Bavra** after Vardzia to reduce Tbilisi backtracking. [BMEIA Georgia](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/reise-services/reiseinformation/land/georgien) · [Georgia-Armenia crossings](https://maspindzeli.ge/guides/georgia-armenia-azerbaijan-borders/).
- **Safety:** Do not use Armenia's **Ijevan–Noyemberyan M16/H26** road or approach the Azerbaijani border; the FCDO advises against the route and within 5km of eastern border. [FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/armenia/regional-risks).

## Why this order wins

The three-country southern Caucasus is NOT a three-sided road loop. Entering via **Baku first** and proceeding west through Sheki allows the only potentially direct Azerbaijan→Georgia continuation for an Austrian traveler. The longer Georgian loop for Kazbegi and Svaneti is the price of actually seeing the mountains. Exit Georgia at Bavra after Vardzia into Gyumri rather than deadheading all the way back to Tbilisi. **Finish Yerevan** so the remote Tatev circuit is before a safe airport hotel night.

**If Balakan outbound road exit fails:** Sheki→Baku (~5–7h) + Baku→Tbilisi flight + Tbilisi→Kakheti: budget another 1–2 days and €70–200+ depending on flight day/booking. If Azerbaijani land exit is conclusively closed for your nationality, the optimal plan changes to **Baku→Sheki→Baku→fly Tbilisi**. The on-map Sheki→Lagodekhi connector is conditional.
