## Map-data limitations

The itinerary's GeoJSON contains geocoded **approximate place-centre/POI waypoints** (where precise OSM/UNESCO coordinates were available, attributed per feature). A portion of city and rural stop coordinates are approximate orientation references, **not a fully audited navigation-quality geocoding database**. Road and walking lines are intentionally **straight-line planning connectors** with transport semantics, not OSRM routing or verified trail GPX. For actual hiking, download the **Transcaucasian Trail/other up-to-date GPX** and check local conditions. Static SVG uses the repository's Basemap/GSHHS renderer; no generative geography.
