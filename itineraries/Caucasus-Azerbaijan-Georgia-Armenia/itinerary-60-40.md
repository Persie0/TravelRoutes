# 60:40 route — 12 days, Georgia + Armenia

**Goal:** about 60% of normalized memorable-experience value in ~40% of the full-trip time; this 12-day geometric optimum reaches a measured-model estimate around **54%** and explicitly documents the shortfall. Here 12/28=43% (close to the target). This is a **different trip**, not a rushed attempt to visit all three countries. The longest geographical sacrifice is **all of Azerbaijan**, which cuts one extra flight and a difficult asymmetric border crossing. The second sacrifice is the **full four-day Svaneti walk**; a weather-permitting 4×4 visit preserves the Ushguli landmark but **not** the trekking experience. Tatev and the Haghpat detour are trimmed. A transparent additive model (per-sight value × uniqueness / 10 × tier weight, with S=1.5, A=1, B=0.4, C=0.1) estimates **about 54% of Full's scored experience** if optional Ananuri and Gelati are reached, and **about 56% of S/A-tier score**. This **falls short of the aspirational 60%**, reflecting the long transfer overhead of two countries and Svaneti. A closer-to-60% version would take approximately **14–15 days**, adding Haghpat/Sanahin and Tatev. These are editorial planning scores, not measured utilities.

| Day | Sleep | Sights / transport |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tbilisi | Arrive, Old Town, sulphur baths / evening viewpoints |
| 2 | Stepantsminda | Mtskheta + Ananuri en route; Gergeti evening if clear |
| 3 | Stepantsminda | Gergeti sunrise + Juta/Chaukhi hike; sleep Kazbegi |
| 4 | Kutaisi | Travel Kazbegi → Tbilisi → Kutaisi; late city walk |
| 5 | Mestia | Early road transfer; tower villages / preparation |
| 6 | Mestia | 4×4 day trip Ushguli and Shkhara views; return same evening or sleep Ushguli if road logistics favor it |
| 7 | Kutaisi | Return from Svaneti; optional Gelati if transport allows |
| 8 | Akhaltsikhe | Kutaisi → Borjomi → Akhaltsikhe; optional Rabati |
| 9 | Gyumri | Morning Vardzia, then cross Ninotsminda/Bavra to Gyumri |
| 10 | Yerevan | Gyumri short look, drive Yerevan; Cascade at sunset |
| 11 | Yerevan | Garni, Geghard and central Yerevan; dinner |
| 12 | Yerevan / Home | Khor Virap and Noravank only if **overnight flight next day**; otherwise do one and allow airport buffer |


**Bookability caution:** day 12 cannot contain a full Noravank return and an early flight. If the flight is early, substitute Yerevan morning; add a **day 13** for full Noravank + departure buffer. Svaneti 4×4 access is mountain-weather dependent. Cross-border car services need prior confirmation.

**If trekking is your top priority**, the better 60:40 alternative is **12–13 days just in Georgia**: Tbilisi → Kazbegi/Juta → Mestia → four-day Ushguli trek → Kutaisi → Tbilisi. It gives up Armenia but keeps the region's most valuable long trail.
