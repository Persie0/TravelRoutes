## Two distinct winners

**Raw cheapest airfare for a coherent month-long example:** 1 Sep BUD→GYD **€91** + 30 Sep EVN→BUD **€84** = **€175 per person** (two direct one-ways, assuming fares still exist and no extras). Research snapshot only; these are NOT guaranteed paired booking prices.

**Best total value:** BUD→GYD + EVN→BUD if train cost is low and flight hours are convenient. Budget **~€20–€60 each way** for Vienna↔Budapest advance rail/transfer and a further airport-transfer cost; a separate overnight hotel could erase much of the difference. Vienna–Budapest rail typically takes ~2h40 city-centre to city-centre, *plus Budapest airport access* ([Seat61](https://www.seat61.com/trains-and-routes/vienna-to-budapest-by-train.htm)). A direct EVN→VIE can be attractive when price is close and arrival time better.

**Fair cost comparison** (indicative): BUD open-jaw raw €175 → perhaps **€235–€330+** after train/airport positioning *before checked luggage*. A pure VIE pair for **matching first/last dates was not quoted** by the cheapest-month lookup, so **do not label Budapest universally cheapest all-in**. Wizz base fare may exclude cabin trolley and checked bag; compare with Austrian/Pegasus baggage-inclusive offers.

## Other shells

1. **Reverse EVN→Baku** needs a **Georgia→Azerbaijan flight** (not a normal land crossing), making it inferior unless flights and border rules materially change.
2. **BUD/VIE→Tbilisi, EVN→BUD/VIE** skips Azerbaijan and saves the border plus multiple days: best for 60:40.
3. **Return from Kutaisi (KUT)** is attractive if doing Georgia only; much less attractive after southern Armenia.
4. **Tbilisi↔Baku flight fallback:** if Sheki→Lagodekhi exit is not operating for the traveler at trip time, return to Baku and fly to Tbilisi. Confirm viable flights and visa entry conditions before committing to rural stays.

## Recheck before purchasing

Airline/flight number/weekday; separate-ticket missed-connection risk; visa name spelling; luggage (including 4-day trek pack); Budapest airport transportation (BUD is not the rail station); entry restrictions; minimum transfer times; and whether dates actually align with the *same* 28/30-day itinerary.
