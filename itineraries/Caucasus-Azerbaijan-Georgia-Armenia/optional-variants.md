# Material route variants

| Variant | Duration | What changes | When it wins |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Recommended 3-country full** | **28–30 days** | Baku→Sheki→Georgia→Armenia, 4-day Svaneti hike | September, hiking + cultures |
| **Georgia + Armenia focused** | **12–17 days** | Skip Azerbaijan, fly into Tbilisi/Kutaisi and out Yerevan | Cost / time saving; fewer border unknowns |
| **Georgia hiking heavy** | **14–18 days** | Kazbegi/Juta/Truso + full Mestia→Ushguli trek and extra mountain weather day; optional Racha | Nature is the priority |
| **Azerbaijan mountains extended** | **30–33 days** | Add Quba/Khinalig from Baku before Lahij; 1–2 extra days and long detour | High-mountain culture and UNESCO desire |
| **Culture / archaeology heavy** | **26–29 days** | Replace 4-day trek with Ushguli 4×4; add Uplistsikhe, Mtskheta, Haghpat, Geghard and more city museums | Less strenuous, more heritage |
| **Relaxed / low transit fatigue** | **32–35 days** | Split Kazbegi→Kutaisi, Gyumri→Dilijan, Ushguli→Kutaisi; add two trail weather buffers | Less transfer risk |
| **Coast / nightlife extension** | **+2–3 days** | Kutaisi→Batumi/Black Sea→Kutaisi (or exit Kutaisi) | Beach days matter, though September coast weather varies |
| **All public transport budget** | **30–34 days** | Add slack around Lahij, Sheki exit, Bavra, Haghpat and Svaneti return | If splitting private driver fares is difficult |

Do not plan an Azerbaijan entry by land or an Armenia–Azerbaijan crossing without a new official rule expressly permitting it. The Georgian mountains are significantly more rewarding than an arbitrary equal split of nights among three capitals.
