## Major trade-offs

- **Khinalig** is remarkable but westward exit via Sheki makes it a 1–2 day out-and-back detour. Add it only in a 30–32-day geography/nature variant; remove Lahij or a city day if time is fixed.
- **Tusheti/Omalo** is a weather-sensitive 4×4 access detour. Not worth displacing the core four-day Svaneti trek on a first trip. [Georgia Travel](https://georgia.travel/).
- **Batumi** adds sea/coast but weakens the autumn mountain-first route; add 2–3 days only if beach/nightlife matters.
- **Truso** and **Juta** are both excellent; the baseline includes Juta and treats Truso as the weather/fitness swap. Fit hikers with 30 days can do both.
- **Haghpat/Sanahin** are academically important UNESCO monuments, but the Gyumri → Lori → Dilijan travel day is extremely long. Overnight in Alaverdi or remove if public transport-only.
- **Tatev** is far from Yerevan but distinct enough to merit the 2-day side circuit via Goris. Check up-to-date advisories for Syunik and do not enter restricted border zones.
