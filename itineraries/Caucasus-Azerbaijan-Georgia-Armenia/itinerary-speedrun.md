# Speedrun — 23 scheduled days (recommended 24–25)

**Objective:** retain essentially all full-route S/A sights and all **four walking stages** of the Svaneti trek; trim sightseeing slack, duplicative evenings, and most weather buffers. This is not the 60:40 itinerary. Theoretical physical minimum is **22–23 days** if border, trail, cross-country rides and early flights line up; **24–25 days recommended** for one to two weather/logistics contingency days. The schedule below has **23 operational days**, with at least one floating recovery/border day strongly recommended.

## Minimum useful dwell-time audit

| Base | Minimum useful time | S/A retained | Tactic | Skipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baku + Gobustan | 1.5–2d | Old City, Gobustan | Reserve full day; early departure | Museum extras |
| Lahij + Sheki | 1.5–2d | Sheki Khan Palace, Lahij craft | Private car in rural sections | Leisurely countryside |
| Kakheti + Tbilisi | 1.5d | Wine landscape, Old Town | Evening city sightseeing | Second Tbilisi day |
| Kazbegi | 1.5–2d | Gergeti, Juta/Chaukhi | Private shuttle for Juta | Truso as B-tier alternative |
| Kutaisi + Mestia transfers | ~2d | Gelati if feasible, Svan towers | Early direct shuttle | Prometheus Cave |
| Mestia→Ushguli trek | **4 full hiking days** | All four stages | Guesthouse prebooking, GPX | No hiking stages skipped |
| Vardzia + Gyumri | ~1.5d | Vardzia caves | Driver via Bavra border | Long Borjomi stroll |
| Lori + Dilijan + Sevan | ~1.5–2d | Haghpat, Sanahin, Sevan | Planned driver, no M16 detour | Dilijan leisurely day |
| Yerevan + Garni + Geghard | 1.5d | Geghard UNESCO, Garni, Cascade | Combine in a long day | Most museums |
| Noravank + Tatev | 2d | Khor Virap, Noravank, Tatev | Overnight Goris | Extra canyon stops |


**High-risk dependencies:** (1) Azerbaijan exit/checkpoint 2027, (2) Adishchala river crossing and high-pass fog, (3) 4×4 Ushguli exit road, (4) rural Georgia–Armenia land border and transport. Put the **first floating buffer in Svaneti**, then at the Azerbaijan exit or Tatev return as needed. If a crossing is impossible, add Baku→Tbilisi flight fallback and increase duration.
