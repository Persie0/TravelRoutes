## Advance booking sequence

1. Before non-refundable flights: cross-check **Azerbaijan exit** rules and exact nationality/checkpoint, Georgia entry insurance, and Armenia safety advisories. If uncertain, price the Baku→Tbilisi flight fallback or choose Georgia/Armenia only.
2. Once flights are live and correctly dated, reserve **Svaneti guesthouses 16–19 Sep** and a 4×4 pickup from Ushguli. Keep trek days movable if weather closes a pass; reserve lodging flexibly.
3. Organize **Azerbaijan e-visa** from [official ASAN portal](https://www.evisa.gov.az/en/countries) sufficiently ahead; the official 2026 fees are USD20 state + USD9 service and may change.
4. Obtain Georgia travel health/accident insurance with **≥30,000 GEL coverage**, English/Georgian document, for the *full Georgia stay*; hiking rescue is a separate practical policy concern. [Georgian government](https://www.geoconsul.gov.ge/en/news/69).
5. Arrange high-friction leg **Vardzia → Bavra → Gyumri** ahead, but avoid paying for unsupported through-service; check road and border.
6. Reconfirm **Tatev / southern Armenia** safety and ropeway operating days; sleep Yerevan on 29 Sep before departure.
