# Caucasus — general optimized route (~28 days)

### Decision

**Go in September** and travel **east → west → south**: fly **Baku** → **Sheki** → **outbound-only Georgia border** → **Kakheti/Tbilisi** → **Kazbegi/Juta** → **Kutaisi/Svaneti** → **Borjomi/Vardzia** → **Gyumri/Lori/Dilijan** → **Yerevan/Garni/Tatev** → fly home.

The route includes unavoidable return movements at Kazbegi and Svaneti because they are mountain spurs. The big optimization is **not** returning Sheki → Baku for a flight if legal Azerbaijan outbound land exit is functioning. An alternative flight exit through Baku is documented for changed restrictions.

**Suggested booking window:** 1–30 September 2027, while the evergreen core is 28 days; place 2 extra days around Svaneti and immediately before international departure. Aim to trek no later than about the middle/end of September (weather varies). Calendar dates are illustrative, not reservations.
