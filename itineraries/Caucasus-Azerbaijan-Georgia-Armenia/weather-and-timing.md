# Weather & timing: month-by-month travel suitability

**Best single month: September. Second-best: late August (mountains) / early October (lowlands, but not a reliable 4-day high trek).** Best-value shoulder season: **early September** or late June for trekking, although weather is less stable in June. Scores are subjective 1–10 **activity suitability**, not measured climate statistics or a daily weather forecast.

## Date logic

Prioritize Svaneti's four-day walking block in **early to mid September**, with one floating buffer. If extending toward October, spend those dates in low-elevation Baku, Tbilisi, Yerevan or Vayots Dzor rather than gambling on high passes. September remains warm in Baku; bring water for Gobustan. Higher Svaneti villages can be cold at night despite sunny afternoons. ## Risks

- Fog or rain can ruin Gergeti/Juta views for one day; swap order of Kazbegi activities.
- **Adishi river** rises with glacier melt; crossing by horse is often the sensible solution. Weather and snow/ice can close higher paths: carry GPX and check locally.
- Ushguli↔Mestia 4×4 roads and tiny Svaneti flights are weather-dependent. Never make the sole flight home rely on a same-day Svaneti transfer.
- Azerbaijan road sections in Lahij and Georgia's mountain highways can take substantially longer than map estimates. Build reserves at checkpoints.
- In extreme heat, do Gobustan early, then shaded indoor Baku sights; in autumn rain use Kutaisi caves/museums as plan B.
