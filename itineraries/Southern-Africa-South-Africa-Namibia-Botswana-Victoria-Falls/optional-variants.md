# Worthwhile route variants

## 1. Relaxed 6–7 week version

Add 5–8 days to the 42-day route:

- +1 Cape Town weather buffer;
- +1 Garden Route coast day;
- +1 Swakopmund recovery/activity day;
- +1 Damaraland wildlife day;
- +1 Moremi/Khwai night;
- +1 Chobe night;
- +1 Victoria Falls/Zambia-side day.

Best if the trip should feel like a journey rather than a checklist.

## 2. Maximum-sightseeing ~49 days

Add **southern Namibia** only if willing to accept the detour:

Cape Town → Fish River Canyon → Lüderitz/Kolmanskop → Sossusvlei → continue north.

This is the strongest “see nearly everything” variant, but it is much less efficient than flying Cape Town→Windhoek and starting the Namibia loop at Sossusvlei.

## 3. Safari-heavy

Cut Garden Route + Winelands. Add:

- extra Etosha night;
- extra Mahango/Bwabwata night;
- Nxai/Makgadikgadi;
- +1 Moremi and +1 Khwai;
- Chobe 2 nights.

## 4. Hiking/coast-heavy

Keep Garden Route and add:

- two Table Mountain hikes;
- Cape of Good Hope overnight trail if permits/logistics suit;
- Tsitsikamma Waterfall/Otter Trail section;
- more Spitzkoppe walking;
- less Nxai/Savuti repetition.

## 5. No remote self-drive

For travelers uncomfortable with expedition 4×4:

Cape Town → Windhoek self-drive Namibia → return/hand over vehicle in Maun or Kasane depending rental → **guided/fly-in Okavango/Moremi** → fly Maun→Kasane → Chobe → shuttle Victoria Falls.

This costs more but materially reduces risk and navigation/mechanical burden.

## 6. Budget-conscious

- use South Africa normal rental, not 4×4;
- fly CPT→WDH only when competitive with bus after time value;
- Namibia camping 4×4/camper;
- use Maun-based mokoro + guided day/short mobile safari rather than premium fly-in lodges;
- prioritize Chobe boat cruise over expensive private concessions;
- Victoria Falls town guesthouse + self-guided official viewpoints.

Do **not** economize by taking an unsuitable 2WD into remote Botswana tracks.

## 7. Victoria Falls high-water version

Travel roughly **May–June**. Keep the same route, but allocate two nights at the falls and consider a helicopter flight if spray obscures ground views at peak flow.

## 8. Low-water adventure version

Travel **late August–October** for top dry-season safari conditions and potentially low-water activities such as Devil's Pool (official opening required). Expect Victoria Falls to look less like a continuous wall of water, especially as the dry season progresses.
