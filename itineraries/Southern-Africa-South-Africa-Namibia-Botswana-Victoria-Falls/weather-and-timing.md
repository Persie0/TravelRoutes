# Weather and timing

The ideal month is a compromise between **Cape weather, Namibia road/safari conditions, Botswana wildlife/access, and Victoria Falls water level**. There is no single month that maximizes all four.

## Best overall choice: late August–September

Why:

- Namibia's Etosha game viewing is strongest in the dry period; Namibia Tourism specifically highlights **May–October** for game viewing.
- Botswana's northern parks are in the classic dry-season wildlife pattern; Chobe's official tourism page highlights large elephant/buffalo concentrations at the river in the dry winter months.
- Remote Botswana tracks are generally more predictable than during the rains.
- Cape Town is transitioning toward spring by September.

Trade-off: Victoria Falls is lower than its flood-season maximum.

## Best choice if Victoria Falls water volume matters most: June–July

Zambia Tourism describes **March–June** as Victoria Falls' prime flood season. June gives a particularly good compromise because Namibia/Botswana dry-season conditions are already strong. July is slightly later and still generally a better waterfall-volume compromise than September.

## Best-value shoulder month: May

May often offers:

- strong residual Victoria Falls flow;
- beginning of the preferred dry safari season;
- cooler Namibia conditions;
- potentially less peak-pressure than July–September.

## Activity-specific caveats

### Devil's Pool

This is a **low-water activity**, not a fixed calendar guarantee. Zambia Tourism material lists it in the low-water period roughly **August–December**; operators/opening authorities must confirm safe access. Never build the trip around an assumed opening date.

### Cape weather

Cape Town has a Mediterranean pattern, so June–August can bring rain/wind. Keep Table Mountain/Lion's Head scheduling flexible and use clear weather windows rather than assigning the mountain permanently to one day.

### Botswana green season

November–April is not “bad”: landscapes are greener, birding and births/migrations can be excellent, and Nxai/Makgadikgadi can become more interesting. But remote track conditions are less predictable for a first 4×4 traverse.

## Recommendation hierarchy

1. **Late Aug–Sep:** safari/road-trip first.
2. **Jun–Jul:** best compromise if Victoria Falls is equally important as safari.
3. **May:** shoulder/value choice.
4. **Oct:** excellent wildlife concentration but hotter; falls can be low.
5. **Nov–Mar:** choose intentionally for green-season wildlife/birding rather than as default first-trip timing.
