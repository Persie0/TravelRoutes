This is the version to choose when the goal is to experience the strongest first-trip combination without converting the journey into a sequence of transfer days.

## Why 42 days works

The ideal route deliberately alternates high-effort wilderness blocks with lower-intensity reset days. The two hardest sections are **Etosha→Kavango** and especially **Moremi→Khwai→Savuti**. Removing every buffer produces only a superficially “efficient” itinerary; in practice it increases fatigue, makes mechanical/weather delays dangerous to the schedule, and reduces the benefit of expensive safari nights.

## Vehicle split

1. **South Africa:** normal rental car is sufficient for the Western Cape/Garden Route.
2. **Cape/Garden Route → Windhoek:** fly instead of carrying a cross-border rental thousands of kilometres.
3. **Windhoek → Kasane:** proper 4×4 with high clearance, two spare tyres if available, recovery kit, compressor, adequate fuel/water, and written cross-border authorization.
4. **Kasane → Victoria Falls:** shuttle/transfer after returning the 4×4.

For inexperienced remote self-drivers, replace Days 32–38 with a **guided mobile safari or fly-in combination** rather than treating Moremi/Savuti tracks like ordinary roads.
