## Booking order

The scarce inventory is not the city hotels; it is the **remote Botswana campsites/lodges and suitable 4×4**. Practical booking order:

1. Moremi / Khwai / Savuti nights or guided mobile safari.
2. Windhoek→Kasane one-way 4×4 and cross-border permissions.
3. International open-jaw flight shell.
4. Victoria Falls and Chobe accommodation/activities.
5. Etosha camps.
6. Sossusvlei/Sesriem.
7. Cape Town and remaining city/coast nights.

## Failure-safe option

If remote-track conditions, experience or campsite availability make the self-drive chain impractical, do **Maun → 3–4 night guided Moremi/Khwai safari → fly Maun→Kasane → Chobe → Victoria Falls**. You lose Savuti overland continuity but protect the highest-value safari content and reduce mechanical risk.
