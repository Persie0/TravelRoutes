# Speedrun itinerary — South Africa → Namibia → Botswana → Victoria Falls

## Goal

Keep essentially all major S/A-tier landscapes and safari experiences while cutting duplicate safari days, city slack and low-value recovery time. Unlike the 60:40 route, the Speedrun still keeps the overland Namibia→Botswana spine and the Moremi→Khwai→Savuti traverse.

- Ideal full route: ~42 days
- Strong compressed route: 28 days
- 60:40: ~17 days, major regions/overland sections cut
- **Speedrun theoretical minimum: ~23 days**
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum: 25 days**

The extra two recommended days are not luxury: remote 4×4 tracks, park gate times, border crossings and wildlife timing make an ultra-tight schedule brittle.

## Important experiences retained

Cape Town/Table Mountain, Cape Peninsula, a compressed Garden Route/Tsitsikamma taste, Sossusvlei/Deadvlei, Sandwich Harbour, Spitzkoppe, Twyfelfontein/Damaraland, Etosha, Mahango/Bwabwata, Okavango, Moremi, Khwai, Savuti, Chobe and Victoria Falls.

## Speedrun rules

1. Remote Botswana campsites and 4×4 availability are the schedule anchor; book them before city hotels.
2. Do not schedule remote-track arrival after dark merely to save a night.
3. Respect park gate times, fuel range and border operating hours; these are hard constraints.
4. If self-drive conditions or experience make Moremi/Khwai/Savuti unsafe, replace the traverse with a guided mobile safari rather than forcing the timetable.
5. The 23-day theoretical version removes Day 24 and compresses the Garden Route further; it is much less failure-tolerant.

**Speedrun removes repeat safari days and urban slack, not the iconic desert–delta–savanna sequence.**
