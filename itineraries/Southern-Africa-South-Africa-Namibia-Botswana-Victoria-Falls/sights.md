## S-tier experiences protected in every serious version

- **Okavango + Moremi:** water and land safari in one ecosystem; Botswana Tourism calls Moremi the only officially protected part of the Okavango and a top self-drive destination.
- **Sossusvlei/Deadvlei:** the trip's strongest non-wildlife landscape; the Namibia Tourism Board describes Deadvlei/Sossusvlei as a must-see Namib landscape.
- **Victoria Falls:** a natural route endpoint after Chobe rather than a detour.
- **Table Mountain + Cape Peninsula:** extremely high experience density at the beginning of the trip.
- **Chobe boat safari:** not redundant with Moremi/Etosha because the river perspective and elephant concentrations are the point.
- **Etosha:** easier self-drive safari contrasts with the wetter, rougher Botswana wilderness.
- **Sandwich Harbour:** protects the signature Namib dunes-meet-Atlantic scene.

## First cuts when time is short

1. **Makgadikgadi/Nxai** — beautiful and unique, but requires extra Maun road days; Nxai is kept only in the 42-day version.
2. **Garden Route** — excellent but four days can be reallocated to Botswana wilderness in a 28-day trip.
3. **Spitzkoppe/Damaraland** — high quality, but Sossusvlei and Etosha remain stronger time-for-value anchors.
4. **Tsodilo** — excellent rock art, but it loses to Okavango/Moremi under a tight schedule.
5. **Winelands** — easy to add to Cape Town if pace allows, but not worth sacrificing an S-tier wilderness day.

## Deliberate exclusions

**Kruger:** world-class safari, but geographically inefficient here and partly repetitive with Etosha + Moremi + Chobe.  
**Fish River Canyon/Lüderitz/Kolmanskop:** strong southern-Namibia loop but pulls the route in the wrong direction.  
**Kgalagadi:** a destination trip of its own; too far south-west for this northbound route.  
**Johannesburg:** use as an air hub only if fare economics win.
