# 60:40 highlights route — ~17 days

Goal: preserve roughly the best **60%+ of total normalized sightseeing/experience value in ~40% of the ideal 42-day time**.

Estimated preserved normalized experience score: **~68%**. This is intentionally not “every second stop”; it spends money on strategic flights to remove low-value transit.

## Route

**Cape Town → fly Windhoek → Sossusvlei → Walvis/Sandwich Harbour → Etosha → Divundu/Mahango → Maun/Okavango → Moremi → fly Maun→Kasane → Chobe → Victoria Falls.**

## Biggest sacrifices

The biggest loss is the **feeling of continuous wilderness travel** through Damaraland and Moremi→Khwai→Savuti. The compact route sees more of the highlights as discrete experiences. For a one-off 17-day trip that is the correct trade: it preserves the moments, not every kilometre between them.
