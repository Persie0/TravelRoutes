## What to book first

1. International open-jaw flight.
2. Uyuni→San Pedro 3-day expedition with a reputable operator and clear wet-season/border contingency.
3. Lima→Arequipa and Calama→Santiago flights.
4. Colca accommodation/transport and any fixed Titicaca tour.
5. La Paz→Sucre flight only after confirming the currently operating schedule.

## What to keep flexible

- exact dedicated mirror excursion date in Uyuni;
- San Pedro astronomy night;
- the optional Cerro Rico mine tour;
- La Paz buffer-day excursion;
- one of the San Pedro excursions if weather or a blockade costs a day.
