## The highest-value geographic cluster

The route's strongest continuous section is:

**Uyuni mirror → Laguna Colorada / Eduardo Avaroa → Sol de Mañana → Hito Cajón → San Pedro → Valle de la Luna → Piedras Rojas.**

That cluster is why the 60:40 itinerary still feels like a major trip rather than a random shortened version.

## Sights I would not add to the core month

### Amazon / Rurrenabaque

Excellent destination, but geographically opposite the southeast route and introduces flights, humidity/health prep and several days. Better as a separate Bolivia trip or a 5–7 day extension.

### Sajama National Park

High-quality nature and Bolivia's highest peak area, but it sits west of the route and competes with an already landscape-heavy itinerary. Add only in a hiking-heavy variant.

### Oruro

Useful transport hub and famous for Carnival, but outside Carnival its first-trip sightseeing value is lower than Sucre/Potosí/Titicaca.

### Santa Cruz / Samaipata

Worthwhile, especially if a cheap VVI fare wins, but it creates east–west repositioning and is omitted from the default linear route.

## UNESCO anchors

The route deliberately includes three major Bolivian UNESCO World Heritage experiences without serious detours:

- **Tiwanaku: Spiritual and Political Centre of the Tiwanaku Culture**;
- **Historic City of Sucre**;
- **City of Potosí**.

Potosí is on UNESCO's World Heritage in Danger list because of concerns including Cerro Rico mining impacts, which is another reason not to treat the mine itself as a casual theme-park attraction.
