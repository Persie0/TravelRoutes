# Worthwhile variants

Only variants that materially change the route are included.

## 1. Mirror-max — February

**Use when:** the water reflection is overwhelmingly the #1 goal.

Route stays the same, but put **3 nights in Uyuni** before the 3-day crossing and reduce Santiago/Sucre by a day if needed. Target roughly **1–28 February**.

**Pros:** highest mirror odds.  
**Cons:** highest risk of flooded sectors, route changes and summer storms on the high-altiplano tracks.

## 2. Reliability-first — early March

**Use when:** you still want a realistic water-mirror chance but dislike weather-disrupted 4×4 plans.

Target roughly **20 February–20 March**, reaching Uyuni around **1–5 March**.

**Pros:** transition toward drier conditions; roads often improve.  
**Cons:** mirror probability declines as March progresses; no guarantee of standing water.

## 3. Bolivia-heavy / Madrid–Santa Cruz flight variant

**Use when:** a MAD↔VVI nonstop fare plus VIE/BUD positioning is dramatically cheaper.

**Santa Cruz → Samaipata → Sucre → Potosí → Uyuni → San Pedro → Santiago**.

Skip Peru. This removes Colca/Titicaca but adds low-altitude Bolivia and the Samaipata region. It is geographically less elegant than the Lima entry but can win on airfare.

## 4. Nature / hiking-heavy

Add **Sajama National Park** between La Paz and Sucre/Potosí, ideally 3–4 days.

**Pros:** volcanoes, high-altitude wetlands, hot springs, much quieter than Uyuni.  
**Cons:** high altitude, road logistics, more weather exposure; requires cutting city days or extending beyond a month.

## 5. Amazon extension

Add **Rurrenabaque / Madidi / pampas** for 5–7 days via La Paz flight.

**Pros:** completely different ecosystem, huge biodiversity contrast.  
**Cons:** geographical backtracking; turns a coherent Andes route into a broader Bolivia sampler. Best with 5+ weeks.

## 6. Budget overland

Replace Lima→Arequipa and La Paz→Sucre flights with buses.

**Pros:** lower headline transport cost.  
**Cons:** loses ~1–2 useful days, increases fatigue, and the Bolivia night-bus option conflicts with current BMEIA advice to avoid night overland bus travel. Not recommended unless daytime schedules work and savings are substantial.

## 7. 60:40 maximum-value route

Use the dedicated 12-day itinerary:

**Lima → La Paz → Tiwanaku → Uyuni → mirror → Sur Lípez → San Pedro → Santiago**.

Spend extra on LPB→UYU and CJC→SCL flights to protect sightseeing time.

## 8. Dry-season photography variant

Travel **May/June or September**, keep the route but stop optimizing for water.

**Pros:** crisp skies, much better road reliability, easier high-altiplano logistics.  
**Cons:** Salar is normally the bright-white polygonal salt landscape, not the giant mirror.

## Which variant I would pick

For a first one-month trip built around the user's stated water preference: **the default late-February/early-March open-jaw route** remains the best overall balance. The only variant likely to beat it is the Santa Cruz/Madrid version if actual long-haul airfare is several hundred euros cheaper on the final dates.
