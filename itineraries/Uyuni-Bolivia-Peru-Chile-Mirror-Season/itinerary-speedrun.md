# Speedrun itinerary — Peru → Bolivia → Uyuni → Atacama

## Goal

Keep essentially all important S/A-tier cultural, altitude, mirror-season and Atacama experiences while removing redundant city days and soft buffers. Unlike the 60:40 route, this version still keeps Colca, Titicaca, Sucre and Potosí.

- Full route: 30 days
- 60:40: ~12 days, several major regions cut
- **Speedrun theoretical minimum: ~21 days**
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum: 23 days**

The two extra recommended days are important because altitude acclimatization, Bolivian roadblocks, Uyuni mirror weather and the Hito Cajón border are real constraints. A Speedrun should be fast, not medically stupid.

## Important experiences retained

Lima historic/coastal gateway, Arequipa, Colca/condors, Titicaca/Taquile, Copacabana/Isla del Sol, La Paz cable cars, Tiwanaku, Sucre, Potosí, dedicated Uyuni mirror day, full 3-day Sur Lípez crossing, Valle de la Luna, Piedras Rojas/altiplanic lagoons, El Tatio, astronomy opportunity and Santiago departure buffer.

## Speedrun rules

1. Never delete the gradual altitude ramp merely to save time.
2. Keep the dedicated mirror day separate from the 3-day crossing; the mirror is the trip goal and cannot be guaranteed on a transfer timetable.
3. Use daytime Bolivian overland travel where practical and re-check live security/roadblock conditions.
4. Do not book a tight onward flight immediately after Hito Cajón; border/road conditions can change.
5. Move Day 22 backward to Uyuni/La Paz/Potosí if disruption occurs.
6. The 21-day theoretical version removes Day 22 and compresses Lima/Santiago further; it has almost no disruption tolerance.

**Speedrun deletes redundant museums and recovery afternoons, not acclimatization, the mirror day or the Sur Lípez/Atacama core.**
