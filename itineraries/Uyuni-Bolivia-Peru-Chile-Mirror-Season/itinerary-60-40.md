# 60:40 highlights route — 12 days

Goal: keep roughly the best 60%+ of the experience value in about 40% of the 30-day trip.

## Condensed route

**Lima → La Paz → Tiwanaku → Uyuni → Salar mirror day → 3-day Sur Lípez crossing → San Pedro de Atacama → Calama → Santiago**

| Day | Sleep | Plan | Intensity |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lima | Arrive via cheap long-haul gateway; connect onward next day | 🟢 |
| 2 | La Paz | Fly to La Paz; very easy acclimatization day | 🟢 |
| 3 | La Paz | Tiwanaku + cable-car panorama | 🟡 |
| 4 | Uyuni | Fly LPB→UYU when operating; acclimatize / mirror scout | 🟡 |
| 5 | Uyuni | **Dedicated Salar mirror day + sunset** | 🟡 |
| 6 | 4×4 refuge | Start Salar / Sur Lípez expedition | 🔴 |
| 7 | 4×4 refuge | Lagoons + Laguna Colorada | 🔴 |
| 8 | San Pedro | Sol de Mañana → Hito Cajón → San Pedro | 🔴 |
| 9 | San Pedro | Recovery + Valle de la Luna sunset | 🟡 |
| 10 | San Pedro | Piedras Rojas + altiplanic lagoons | 🔴 |
| 11 | Santiago | Bus to Calama + fly to Santiago | 🟡 |
| 12 | — | Fly home | 🟡 |

## Why these stops survive

The mirror Salar, Eduardo Avaroa/Sur Lípez landscapes and Atacama form one unusually efficient trans-border landscape corridor. Tiwanaku adds a genuinely different archaeological experience without a major detour from La Paz.

## Estimated score retention

Using the normalized sight scores in `data/sights.csv`, this version retains about **65–70% of the memorable first-trip value** while using 40% of the time. The sacrifice is breadth—colonial cities, Titicaca and Colca—rather than the highest-uniqueness landscape experiences.

## Important warning

This condensed version has less resilience to altitude symptoms, weather or roadblocks. Add 1–2 days if flights are expensive or if your body does not handle a rapid jump to La Paz well.
