# Flight strategy

Research snapshot: 2026-08-24. Prices are temporary snapshots, not evergreen promises.

## Best route shell

**Vienna (VIE) → London (LON) / Edinburgh (EDI) → Vienna (VIE)** as an open-jaw trip.

This is better than returning from London because the itinerary naturally ends in Edinburgh. The time/cost of positioning back to London can easily erase a nominal airfare saving.

## Current direct-route evidence

- Austrian currently publishes multiple daily **VIE → London Heathrow** non-stop flights, about 2h20–2h30. Its fare calendar showed **July 2027 from €205** for the VIE–London route at the research date.
- Austrian currently publishes a **non-stop Edinburgh → Vienna** route around 2h35; the schedule is not daily on every date and changes seasonally.
- Current schedule aggregators also show Ryanair on EDI–VIE; use this only as a prompt to compare baggage-inclusive totals when your exact dates open.

## What to compare once exact dates are fixed

1. VIE → LHR/LGW/STN + EDI → VIE
2. BUD → London + EDI → VIE
3. VIE → London + EDI → BUD
4. Round-trip VIE ↔ London + separate EDI → London positioning flight/train — **only** if it is materially cheaper after luggage and time

## Raw cheapest vs best total value

A £30–60 cheaper fare into an inconvenient London airport can disappear after airport rail/bus transfers. Likewise, a cheap round trip to London is not automatically better if it forces a 4+ hour Edinburgh→London train plus a hotel/buffer before the flight.

**Default booking preference:** convenient London arrival + direct Edinburgh home, even if the raw airfare is modestly higher.
