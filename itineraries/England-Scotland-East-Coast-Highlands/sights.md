## A-tier — include unless weather/logistics argue otherwise

York walls/Shambles; National Railway Museum; Durham peninsula/castle; Newcastle Quayside; St Andrews ruins/old town; Royal Deeside; Culloden + Clava Cairns; Loch Ness/Urquhart Castle; Achmelvich Bay; Ardvreck Castle; Neist Point; Fairy Pools; Dunvegan; Glenfinnan; Glen Etive/Rannoch Moor; National Museum of Scotland.

## B/C cuts when time gets tight

- Extra Aberdeen city time beyond Old Aberdeen / harbour
- A full Newcastle day if cities are not a priority
- Tynemouth if weather is poor
- Generic whisky stops if nobody cares about whisky
- Repetitive Skye roadside photo stops after Storr + Quiraing + Cuillin are already covered
- John o’Groats as a goal in itself — useful only as part of a wider Caithness/Orkney extension

## Highest value per detour hour

1. Durham Cathedral (on the rail line)
2. Dunnottar Castle (small Stonehaven detour)
3. Stac Pollaidh
4. Quiraing
5. Glenfinnan on the Mallaig→Glencoe route
6. Stirling Castle on the final drive to Edinburgh
