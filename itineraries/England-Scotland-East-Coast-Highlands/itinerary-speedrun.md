# Speedrun itinerary — London → East Coast → Highlands → Edinburgh

## Goal

Keep essentially all important S/A-tier city and Highland experiences while cutting recovery days, duplicate city time and weather slack.

- Full route: 30 days
- 60:40: ~13 days, major regions cut
- **Speedrun theoretical minimum: ~19 days**
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum: 21 days**

The two extra recommended days are weather insurance for Cairngorms / Assynt / Torridon / Skye / Glencoe. A 19-day run is possible only with strong forecasts and very efficient driving.

## 19-day theoretical version

Delete Day 20 and compress Edinburgh Days 18–19 into one extremely long day. This is only sensible if the Highland forecast has already cooperated.

## Important sights retained

London historic/museum core, Oxford, York, Durham, St Andrews, Dunnottar, Cairngorms, Culloden/Clava, Assynt, Torridon, Applecross, Skye north + Cuillin/Elgol, Glenfinnan, Glencoe, Stirling and Edinburgh.

## Speedrun rules

- Pick up the car only in the north-east; rail remains faster for the English city chain.
- Use luggage storage and late trains rather than adding hotel nights for half-day cities.
- Treat Assynt/Torridon/Skye/Glencoe as one weather portfolio: move the flexible day to the region with the best forecast.
- Never force a summit in bad Scottish mountain weather merely to preserve the schedule.

**Speedrun deletes slack, not the Highlands.**
