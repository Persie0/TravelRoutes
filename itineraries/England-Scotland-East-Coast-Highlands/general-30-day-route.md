## Why the buffers matter

The highest-value part of this trip is weather-sensitive. Days 14, 17, 19, 21–23 and 25 should be treated as a **portfolio of outdoor options**, not rigid appointments. If the mountain forecast is poor, swap a hike with a castle, museum, distillery or scenic drive rather than forcing a summit.

## What I intentionally cut

- **Bath** from the core trip: excellent city, wrong direction for this route.
- **Cambridge overnight:** easy to visit, but adds less unique value than another Highland day; use as a London day trip only if preferred over Oxford.
- **Full NC500 east/north arc:** too much extra driving for a first trip unless 35+ days.
- **Glasgow:** worthwhile, but geographically unnecessary when the requested finish is Edinburgh.
