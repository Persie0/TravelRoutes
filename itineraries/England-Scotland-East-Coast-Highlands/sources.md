## Map data / method

- Stop coordinates are stored in `maps/stops.geojson` using standard geographic place centres and cross-checked for relative position/country/island-mainland sanity.
- Coastlines and borders in the SVG use GSHHS/Basemap geographic data.
- Route lines are deliberately labeled **planning connectors** unless routed geometry is supplied. They must not be interpreted as exact road or rail tracks.
- Interactive map tiles: OpenStreetMap contributors via Leaflet; normal end-user interactive loading only.
