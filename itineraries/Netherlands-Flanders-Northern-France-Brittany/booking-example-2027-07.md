Current 2026/27 route evidence supports NTE↔VIE nonstop as an active Volotea city pair, but exact July 2027 operating days and price should be verified before committing non-refundable accommodation.

## July-specific cautions

- French school-holiday / summer demand means **Brittany coastal hotels and rental cars should be booked early**.
- Mont-Saint-Michel and Saint-Malo will be busy: use first/late time slots.
- 14 July can bring closures, transport changes and fireworks/events; Lille is a reasonable place to absorb this rather than putting a critical transfer or museum-only day there.
- Keep at least one western-Brittany booking flexible enough to react to wind/rain.

## What to book in order

1. Return flight strategy / exact NTE operating day.
2. One-way Rouen→Nantes rental car.
3. Saint-Malo / Mont-Saint-Michel-area nights.
4. Perros-Guirec / Crozon accommodation.
5. Bruges weekends.
6. Remaining rail-city hotels.
7. Flexible train tickets where advance fares save meaningfully.
