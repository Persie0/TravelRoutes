## Main conclusions

- **Do not spend a full sightseeing day in Brest at the expense of Crozon or Pointe Saint-Mathieu.**
- **Bruges deserves two nights** even for a fast trip because early/late hours are materially better than the day-trip peak.
- **Amiens is ideal as a through-stop**, not necessarily an overnight.
- **Ypres is the best additional Flemish city for route geometry**; Leuven/Mechelen are good but less efficient here.
- The nature-heavy western half is weather-sensitive, so one flexible day around Côte de Granit Rose/Crozon has more value than adding another medium-priority city.
