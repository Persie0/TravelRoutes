# Worthwhile Variants

## A. 30–32 days with Maastricht

Insert **Rotterdam → Maastricht (2 nights) → Ghent**. Keep the entire Brittany section intact. Maastricht is worth adding only by extending, not by cutting Crozon/Mont-Saint-Michel/Bruges.

Best if you specifically want caves/fortifications, a more southern Dutch atmosphere and another strong old city.

## B. 29 days with Utrecht instead

Add **Utrecht** immediately after arrival or before Haarlem. This is geometrically cleaner than Maastricht and the best single-day Dutch extension.

## C. Nature-heavy 28 days

Cut:

- Haarlem second day;
- Lille museum half-day;
- Ypres overnight (visit briefly or skip).

Add:

- Zeeland / Delta Works;
- extra Côte de Granit Rose day;
- second Crozon hiking day;
- Quiberon or Belle-Île if ferry timing works.

## D. Culture/history-heavy 28 days

Keep Ypres and add more time for:

- Ghent altarpiece + museums;
- Flemish Primitive art in Bruges;
- WWI memorials around Ypres;
- Louvre-Lens or Roubaix architecture as a Lille excursion;
- full Bayeux + two D-Day museums;
- Carnac interpretation/prehistoric sites.

Cut one western-Brittany hiking day if necessary.

## E. Budget February version

Use rail/bus longer and rent a car for only the highest-value Normandy/Brittany segment. Reduce exposed coast days and add more urban/museum time in Haarlem, Leiden, Ghent, Lille, Rouen and Nantes.

Do **not** expect February to deliver the same Crozon/Granite Coast value as September/July.

## F. No-car version

Possible but weaker. Suggested line:

Haarlem → Leiden → Rotterdam → Ghent → Bruges → Lille → Rouen → Bayeux → Mont-Saint-Michel bus/tour → Saint-Malo → Rennes → Nantes.

This avoids a rental but cuts/compromises Étretat-Honfleur pairing, D-Day coast flexibility, Cap Fréhel, Côte de Granit Rose, Crozon and Pointe Saint-Mathieu. For this trip, a car after Rouen has unusually high marginal value.

## G. Stop at Brest and go home immediately

Only do this if a specific BES flight deal is compelling. Current schedules do not offer a Brest→Vienna nonstop. Normal better-value options are:

1. continue sightseeing to Nantes and fly from NTE;
2. Brest→Nantes by train, then fly;
3. Brest→Paris by TGV, then fly.

## H. End in Paris instead of Nantes

Useful if the direct NTE flight days are poor. Keep Vannes/Nantes if time allows, then TGV to Paris the evening before an early flight. Do not schedule a tight train-to-flight connection on the same morning.
