## Coordinate / map verification

Coordinates in `maps/stops.geojson` were checked against public geographic references including GeoNames, OpenStreetMap-derived references, UNESCO coordinates where applicable, and standard city coordinate references. The static SVG uses Basemap/GSHHS coastlines and country borders. Route lines are explicitly planning connectors, not invented turn-by-turn roads.

## Research-quality caveat

This repository intentionally keeps the evergreen itinerary separate from temporary flight and timetable assumptions. If a future airline drops NTE↔VIE, the itinerary geography still works: use Nantes→Paris TGV and fly from Paris, or compare NTE→BUD plus positioning.
