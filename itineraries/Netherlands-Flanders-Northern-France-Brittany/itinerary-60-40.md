# 60:40 Highlights Route — 11 Days

Goal: preserve roughly the best **60%+ of total first-trip experience value in ~40% of the 28-day time**. Because Amsterdam, Brussels, Antwerp and Liège are already known, the compact version does not repeat them.

**Estimated normalized experience retained:** ~64–68%  
**Best transport:** rail through Belgium/northern France + rental car from Rouen + fly home from Nantes/Paris.

## Biggest sacrifices

The compact route loses the **Dutch small-city sequence**, a deeper Bruges/Ghent pace, Flanders Fields detail, and south Brittany (Carnac/Vannes). What it protects are the experiences hardest to substitute elsewhere: Bruges, Mont-Saint-Michel, the Normandy war coast, Saint-Malo, Pink Granite Coast and Crozon.

For a less exhausting compact trip, make it **13 days** by adding one extra Bruges/Ghent day and one south-Brittany/Nantes day.
