# Speedrun itinerary — Netherlands → Flanders → Northern France → Brittany

## Goal

Keep nearly all important S/A-tier city, war-history and coastal sights while compressing each base to the minimum useful time. Unlike 60:40, this version still keeps the long southwest chain.

- Full route: 28 days
- 60:40: ~11 days, many destinations cut
- **Speedrun theoretical minimum: ~18 days**
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum: 20 days**

The recommended two-day margin protects Étretat/Brittany weather, D-Day timing and Mont-Saint-Michel tide/abbey logistics.

## Important experiences retained

Haarlem, Leiden, Delft, Rotterdam, Kinderdijk, Ghent, Bruges, Ypres/Flanders Fields, Lille, Amiens Cathedral, Rouen, Étretat, Honfleur, Bayeux/D-Day, Mont-Saint-Michel, Saint-Malo, Dinan/Cap Fréhel, Côte de Granit Rose, Crozon, Locronan/Quimper, Carnac/Vannes and Nantes.

## Speedrun rules

1. Use trains until Rouen; rent the car only for Normandy/Brittany.
2. Treat Amiens, Morlaix and Brest as through-stops unless a specific museum is a priority.
3. Book Mont-Saint-Michel around opening hours/tide rather than blindly assigning an hour.
4. Use dawn/evening for Bruges, Saint-Malo and coastal viewpoints; those hours are both prettier and less crowded.
5. Move the flexible day to the worst weather-sensitive coastal segment.

**Speedrun removes hotel moves and duplicate city days before it removes the major coast and heritage chain.**
