## Why not Maastricht in the 28 days?

Maastricht is good enough for a **+2 day extension**, but it is the wrong direction for the route. From Rotterdam you move southeast, then must turn west again to reach Ghent/Bruges. Because you already know Liège, Brussels and Antwerp, there is no obvious eastern-Belgium cluster to make that detour compound into something bigger.

If Maastricht matters personally, insert:

- Day 6 Rotterdam→Maastricht; old town / Vrijthof
- Day 7 Maastricht + caves / fort / river
- Day 8 Maastricht→Ghent

Then shift the rest by two days and target **30 days** rather than cutting Brittany.

## Best places to add days if you have 30–32 days

1. **+1 Utrecht** before Haarlem or after arrival.
2. **+1 Brittany weather buffer** around Perros-Guirec/Crozon.
3. **+1 Maastricht** only if you specifically want it.
4. **+1 South Brittany** for Concarneau/Pont-Aven or Quiberon.

## Best places to cut if you only have 24–25 days

Cut in this order: Haarlem second day → Ypres overnight (make it day trip/through-stop) → Morlaix overnight → Brest overnight. Do **not** cut Bruges, Mont-Saint-Michel, Saint-Malo, D-Day/Bayeux or Crozon first.
