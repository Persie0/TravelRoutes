# Flight Strategy

Research snapshot: **2026-08-24**. Treat all prices and schedules as temporary. Recheck exact days after choosing the month.

## Recommended open-jaw shell

### Outbound

**VIE or BUD → AMS**

Amsterdam is the right arrival airport even though Amsterdam itself is already known: it has abundant competition and excellent rail access to Haarlem/Leiden/Rotterdam.

### Return

**NTE → VIE first choice**  
**NTE → BUD second choice if much cheaper**  
**NTE → Paris by TGV + PAR → VIE as robust backup**

## Why Nantes beats Brest

- Current schedule databases show **no nonstop BES→VIE**.
- Brest→Nantes is roughly **3 h 22 by train** in current Rome2Rio route research.
- Volotea currently sells/markets a **nonstop Nantes↔Vienna** route.
- Volotea's public offers page currently advertises Nantes→Vienna from about **€102.79 return promotional level**; this is only an indicative marketing fare, not a quote for the eventual trip dates.
- easyJet currently operates a limited-weekly **Nantes↔Budapest** nonstop route according to current schedule data.

This makes southern Brittany + Nantes a rare case where **adding more sightseeing also improves the homebound logistics**.

## Raw-cheapest vs total-value logic

A flight that is €40 cheaper from Paris can still lose if it requires:

- a TGV ticket;
- extra airport transfer;
- checked-bag differences;
- an airport hotel or risky same-day connection;
- half a sightseeing day.

Therefore compare:

**fare + positioning transport + baggage + required buffer hotel + lost sightseeing time**.

## Date-flexibility strategy by month

### September — recommended

Search the direct NTE→VIE operating days first, then build the 28-day route backward from the best return date. If the direct route is not operating on the desired September dates, use Paris rather than ending the trip in Brest.

### July / August

Direct seasonal routes are more likely to have useful inventory, but prices and car/hotel costs are usually higher. Book the Brittany rental and high-demand coastal lodging earlier.

### February

Do not assume the same direct seasonal route pattern. Paris is more likely to be the stable fallback. A February version should also be more city-heavy because the coast has shorter daylight and worse weather reliability.

## Airports not worth forcing

- **Brest (BES):** convenient geographically but currently no VIE nonstop.
- **Rennes (RNS):** useful regional airport but less dependable for cheap direct Austria options than simply taking TGV to Paris.
- **Brussels (BRU/CRL):** excellent fares can exist, but returning all the way east after Brittany creates major backtracking.
- **Amsterdam (AMS) return:** only makes sense if a round trip is dramatically cheaper; otherwise it destroys the one-way route geometry.

## Booking rule

When exact dates are chosen, test at minimum:

1. VIE→AMS + NTE→VIE
2. BUD→AMS + NTE→BUD
3. VIE→AMS + NTE→BUD
4. BUD→AMS + NTE→VIE
5. VIE↔AMS round trip + Nantes/Paris positioning
6. Reversed direction only if fares are exceptionally different

Then compare **door-to-door total**, not just headline airfare.
