# Optimized Cuba: complete 28-day first visit

**Evergreen route**, not tied to airline dates. **Conditional plan**, written 18 November 2026. Cuba currently suffers severe fuel/power shortages; Austria specifically advises avoiding travel in eastern provinces affected by Hurricane Melissa. Read [safety + logistics](transport-and-route-optimization.md) and BMEIA before committing to east. This 28-day full-island route is suitable only if transport and official guidance improve. For current conditions prefer the 12-day western/central condensed route *after* the non-essential travel warning has lifted.

## What makes the itinerary worthwhile

- Cultural core: **Havana, Viñales, Trinidad**, with UNESCO Cienfuegos and Camagüey included rather than rushed.
- High-value nature: **Viñales karst, Bay of Pigs reef, Topes de Collantes, Sierra Maestra, Baracoa/Humboldt**. These require flexibility around guide and transport availability.
- True route logic: West loop uses Havana twice, then travels one-way through central Cuba to the east. A single high-cost eastern-to-Havana return is the unavoidable full-island tail when using a Havana round-trip international ticket.
- **No major resort extension** in the core: Varadero, Cayo Santa María and Cayo Coco consume days and fuel for beaches that do not add as much uniqueness as Cuba's historic cities and natural landscapes.

## Critical dependencies / what to reserve

1. Confirm whether **Santiago–Havana domestic flights** or **overnight Viazul** actually operate; the Rome2Rio route overview is *not* proof of a running service. Do not make day 28 international departure dependent on an unconfirmed day 27 domestic movement.
2. Book **Sierra Maestra Commandancia La Plata** and **Alejandro de Humboldt** only through confirmed local guide/park access; during disrupted periods it may be impossible.
3. Keep nights 26–27 movable if possible. When the east is unsafe or inaccessible, replace days 17–27 with **Trinidad + Escambray / Remedios / Cienfuegos + a west-coast beach extension**, not an impossible same-day long drive.
4. Walk from sunrise/early morning where practical; avoid night driving because of road safety, lack of lighting and unreliable fuel.
5. Bring emergency cash, medicines, drinking water plan and offline maps; confirm hotels/casas can actually provide food and water.
