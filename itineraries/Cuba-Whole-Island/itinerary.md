### Booking checkpoints

- **Before any non-refundable purchase:** official BMEIA / Canadian advice and eastern provinces; Cuba-valid insurance; US ESTA implications.
- **6–8 weeks before hypothetical departure:** test whether Viazul, licensed transfers, Sierra Maestra guides and Baracoa–Santiago road connections are operating; no implied guarantees.
- **Early March 2027 only if travel becomes appropriate:** submit tourist eVisa at official Cuba portal; D'Viajeros can be filled **within seven days before landing**, not before.
- **Before international departure:** verify Havana airport connectivity, flight protection, and an overnight buffer after any eastern return.
