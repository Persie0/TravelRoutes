## Price caveats and decision rule

Book only a cancellable/refundable or well-protected itinerary when your country's official advice and the island's infrastructure have improved. Permit at least one full night in Havana before the international departure after an eastern domestic leg, but under unstable transport the better fallback is to **omit eastern Cuba** rather than rely on a flight that may be cancelled. Budget flight taxes, checked baggage and incidental positioning separately.
