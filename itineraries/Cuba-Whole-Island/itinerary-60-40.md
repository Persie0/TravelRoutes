# 60:40 highlights — Cuba in 12 days

**Objective:** maximize Cuba-specific cultural/natural experiences per day, instead of trying to touch every famous town. **12/28 = 42.9%** of the full trip's days (the nearest practical duration once you allow a Havana departure buffer). Presently this is a **future/conditional route only** due to the countrywide infrastructure crisis. Main itinerary stays in the west and central provinces; it omits the long eastern tail.

**Havana → Viñales → Las Terrazas → Havana → Playa Larga / Bay of Pigs → Cienfuegos → El Nicho → Trinidad → Havana.**

## Day by day

| Day | Base | Morning | Afternoon/evening | Movement |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Havana | Land, check into casa, short Old Havana loop | Old Havana and Malecón sunset | No transfer; arrive early if possible |
| 2 | Havana | Start 08:00: plazas, Capitolio, Prado | Brief Vedado stop after Capitolio/Prado; FAC if open | Cluster city sights on foot and short taxis |
| 3 | Viñales | Depart Havana early; Viñales around lunch | Mogote viewpoint, guided tobacco valley | ~3–5h travel; no slow evening arrival |
| 4 | Viñales | Los Acuáticos sunrise viewpoint | Cueva de Santo Tomás if guide/slot, short rural evening | Private local cave transfer |
| 5 | Havana | Viñales→Las Terrazas / Soroa early | Las Terrazas forest/community stop; Soroa waterfall if accessible; return Havana | ~4–7h multi-stop transfer; optional Soroa cut if delays |
| 6 | Playa Larga | Early drive to Zapata; guided wetland birding | Bay of Pigs swimming/snorkeling | ~3–4h road plus local guide |
| 7 | Cienfuegos | Cueva de los Peces / Girón coastal stop | Transfer to Cienfuegos; historic centre | ~2–3h main transfer plus local stops |
| 8 | Trinidad | El Nicho guided waterfall excursion | Continue to Trinidad; evening colonial streets | Road detour ~4–7h; cut El Nicho if poor access |
| 9 | Trinidad | Old-town squares, museums and lanes | Valle de los Ingenios; live music | Compact Trinidad cluster |
| 10 | Trinidad | Early Topes de Collantes guided hike | Playa Ancón late only with enough time; rest/music | Long outdoor day; beach only if daylight/energy |
| 11 | Havana | Morning transfer Trinidad→Havana | Arrival and logistical buffer | ~5–7h road; no same-day long-haul booking |
| 12 | Departure | Breakfast/airport; fly out of Havana | Travel home | Flight day and buffer from prior night |

## What was cut, and why

| Place | Full route | 60:40 | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Havana | 3 days + stopovers | ~2 days + 2 transit nights | Keep the unique old city, living music, compact city core |
| Viñales | 3 days | 2 days | Keep mogotes + sunrise hike + cave if open |
| Las Terrazas / Soroa | Day-7 transit | Short scenic transit stop | Keeps valuable nature with no new overnight |
| Bay of Pigs | 2 days | ~1.5 days | Keep reef and one Zapata outing, drop slower museum time |
| Cienfuegos / El Nicho | ~2 days total | ~1 day + transit | Cluster city and waterfall on the way to Trinidad |
| Trinidad / Escambray | 4 days | ~2 days | Preserve historic city, valley, mountain hike; drop rest time |
| Santa Clara / Remedios | 2 days | 0 | Moderate marginal value on a tight route |
| Camagüey | 1 day | 0 | Good UNESCO site but long move east and then back |
| Bayamo / Sierra Maestra | 2 days | 0 | Two-day mountain logistics cut |
| Santiago / Baracoa / Humboldt | 6+ days | 0 | Outstanding sites but major time/fuel/safety cost |

### 60% experience / 40% time interpretation

Of the 39 selected Full sights in the normalized catalogue, the compressed route retains 22 experiences. The editorial normalized score is **164.6 / 271.6 = 60.6%**, achieved in 12/28 = 42.9% of the time. This assumes the listed venues, nature guides and several tightly clustered optional stops are accessible; if not, realized coverage is lower. See [the scoring catalogue](sights.md) for transparent weights and selection flags. The hard sacrifice is **Santiago + Baracoa/Humboldt**; that is why a 12-day highlights trip is *not* the same as a 23-day Speedrun. The score is not objective evidence that most travelers will experience 60% of every activity.

**Failure plan:** if the early west-to-centre transfers are not available, rebook the long coast leg rather than manufacture impossible Viazul connections. Keep Havana on night 11 so a long-haul flight is not pinned to a five-hour ground transfer.
