# Safety and borders — safety-first scope

Research snapshot: 26 Aug 2026, Austrian BMEIA.

- **Kazakhstan: level 1** — stable overall; normal urban precautions.
- **Kyrgyzstan: level 2** — demonstrations/security incidents possible; avoid political gatherings. The Kyrgyzstan–Tajikistan border conflict was formally settled by bilateral agreement in March 2025, but border-region caution remains sensible.
- **Uzbekistan: level 2** — petty theft and police checks; avoid unnecessary overnight overland travel; extra care near borders and the Fergana region.
- **Tajikistan: level 2 generally, level 3 in GBAO and Afghanistan-border areas** — therefore an optional extension, not core to this safety-first itinerary.

## Border rule

The Kolsai/Saty → Karakol plan depends on a crossing that is open to foreign travellers on your actual date. Recheck immediately before booking the Saty/Karakol transfer. If the direct crossing is not open, retreat to Almaty and use the Korday/Bishkek axis or fly/road around rather than improvising on a remote border.

## General

Register the trip with Austria's foreign-service app if desired; carry passport copies, avoid demonstrations, and do not photograph military/border installations. For mountain days, road/altitude/weather risk is more likely than violent crime: use reputable transfers, do not plan night driving on remote roads, and keep a weather buffer.
