# Flight strategy

## Best shell

Prefer an **open-jaw** ticket structure:

**Vienna or Budapest → Almaty**  
**Urgench or Tashkent → Vienna or Budapest**

This eliminates the need to return across Central Asia after Khiva.

## What to compare when 2027 inventory is mature

1. VIE → ALA / UGC → VIE
2. VIE → ALA / TAS → VIE
3. BUD → ALA / UGC → BUD
4. BUD → ALA / TAS → BUD
5. Round-trip ALA plus Khiva/Tashkent positioning only as a price benchmark

A one-stop itinerary through Istanbul is a natural candidate. Do not sacrifice a full travel day to save a small headline fare: include baggage, airport positioning and the cost of a final domestic flight in the comparison.
