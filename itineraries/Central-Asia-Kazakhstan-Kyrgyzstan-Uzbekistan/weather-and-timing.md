**Requested-month ranking:** 1) September, 2) August, 3) July.

For September, do the mountains first. A 1–28 Sep routing is preferable to 15 Sep–12 Oct if Song-Kul and high hiking are priorities. July/August are excellent for Kyrgyz high country but harsh for Uzbekistan; if travelling then, schedule Uzbek sightseeing at dawn/evening and allow midday downtime.
