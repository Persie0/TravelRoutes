## Key optimization

Do **not** add Astana to this one-month version unless architecture is a major priority: the detour costs a flight or very long rail leg and substitutes poorly for the southeast Kazakhstan nature cluster. Likewise, the Pamir Highway is deliberately excluded because it adds multiple hard travel days and weaker safety margins for this specific brief.
