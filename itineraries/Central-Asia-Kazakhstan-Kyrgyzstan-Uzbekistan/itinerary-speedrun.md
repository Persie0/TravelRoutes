# Speedrun itinerary — Kazakhstan → Kyrgyzstan → Uzbekistan

## Goal

**Speedrun keeps the important S/A-tier experiences and removes slack, not sights.** It is different from the 60:40 route, which deliberately drops whole destinations.

- Full route: 28 days
- 60:40: ~12 days, many stops cut
- **Speedrun theoretical minimum: ~18 days**
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum: 19 days**

The extra recommended day is mainly protection against mountain weather, the Kazakhstan→Kyrgyzstan crossing, Song-Kul road conditions and Uzbek train availability.

## What the Speedrun still protects

- Charyn Canyon
- Kolsai + Kaindy
- Karakol mountain day
- south Issyk-Kul scenery
- Song-Kul yurt/landscape experience
- Ala-Archa
- Tashkent core
- Samarkand
- Bukhara
- Khiva

## Rules

1. Book the Bishkek→Tashkent flight rather than losing a day overland.
2. Use the earliest useful Uzbek high-speed trains.
3. Put mountain days first in the trip and use the flexible Day 19 where the forecast demands it.
4. Do not compress Ala-Kul/Altyn Arashan below a safe route appropriate to fitness/weather.
5. Do not assume the Kazakhstan→Kyrgyzstan crossing or Song-Kul access will behave like a city train connection.

**Speedrun principle:** delete recovery afternoons, duplicate city days and idle lake time before deleting major landscapes or Silk Road cities.
