# Transport and route optimization

## Major legs

| Leg | Recommended | Approx current route time | Why |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Almaty → Karakol | road/bus via open border | ~6h bus (Rome2Rio) | scenic, no flight backtracking |
| Bishkek → Tashkent | **fly** if schedule/pricing works | bus ~12h40; drive ~8h43 | strategic flight saves most of a day |
| Tashkent → Samarkand | high-speed train | ~2h13 | fastest + city-centre to city-centre |
| Samarkand → Bukhara | train | ~2h40 | simple, efficient |
| Bukhara → Khiva | train | ~6h47 | no airport hassle; use early/overnight schedule if available |
| Khiva → Urgench | taxi/transfer | ~40 min | departure gateway |

## International flight shell

Best structure is **open-jaw**: Europe → Almaty, Urgench or Tashkent → Europe. Turkish Airlines currently markets VIE–ALA around the €700+ round-trip level on some 2026 dates and VIE–TAS around €1,000 round-trip on displayed 2026 dates; these are *snapshots, not 2027 forecasts*. Turkish also serves Urgench–Istanbul, making UGC a plausible open-jaw exit. Skyscanner's August 2027 indicative-month query returned no quotes yet, so do not anchor the trip to a fabricated future fare.

Check VIE and BUD once schedules open; compare (a) VIE→ALA / UGC→VIE, (b) VIE→ALA / TAS→VIE, (c) BUD equivalents, and (d) round-trip ALA or TAS plus positioning.
