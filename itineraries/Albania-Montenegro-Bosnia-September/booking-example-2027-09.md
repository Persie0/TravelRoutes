## Documents, safety, mobile money

For **Austrian citizens** on a short tourist stay, BMEIA and the Bosnian foreign ministry list **no tourist visa requirement** for Albania, Montenegro and Bosnia; use a passport valid at least 3 months after departure rather than relying solely on an ID card. Carry extra cash for the Albanian Alps and remote buses. Bosnia uses BAM, Montenegro EUR, Albania ALL; Greece EUR. Check current rules and any wildfire/road-blockade warnings before travel.
