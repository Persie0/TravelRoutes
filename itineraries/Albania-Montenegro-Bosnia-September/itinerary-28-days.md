# 28-day optimized full route — Bosnia, Montenegro and Albania

**Evergreen September plan; sleeping locations, activities, transport and intensity by day.** Example dates: **1–28 September 2027**, assuming suitable flights. September is ideal for crossing Durmitor/Valbonë early and reaching the southern beaches later.

**Direction:** Dubrovnik Airport → Mostar → Konjic → Sarajevo → Plužine/Piva → Žabljak/Durmitor → Kotor → Ulcinj → Shkodër → Koman/Fierzë → Valbonë → Theth → Tirana → Berat → Përmet → Gjirokastër → Himarë → Sarandë/Butrint → Corfu Airport.

**Optimization:** no pointless detour to Dubrovnik old town; the Sarajevo→Plužine→Žabljak section is a **deliberately challenging transfer** that requires a prearranged onward transfer or roadtrip. The alternative Sarajevo→Mostar→Kotor→Žabljak introduces significant backtracking but is easier with conventional long-distance buses. Ferry schedules and hikes are weather/season dependent.

All transfer times below are *indicative door-to-door planning bands*, not verified September 2027 bookings. Border queues, mountain roads and buses can shift the day by several hours.

## Practical notes and optional extras

**Day 01 — Mostar.** Dubrovnik is an airport gateway, not a required extra city.

**Day 02 — Mostar.** Blagaj is worth a half-day; avoid a full guided tour.

**Day 03 — Mostar.** Skip Kravica on low-water days; add Stolac instead.

**Day 04 — Konjic.** Shift to Sarajevo if rafting unavailable.

**Day 05 — Sarajevo.** Rafting is optional, weather- and water-dependent.

**Day 06 — Sarajevo.** One city day is enough if you dislike city-heavy itineraries.

**Day 07 — Sarajevo.** Keep an alternative indoor plan for rain.

**Day 08 — Žabljak.** CRITICAL: do not assume a direct Sarajevo–Žabljak bus; reserve pickup.

**Day 09 — Žabljak.** Use this as weather buffer if tomorrow looks poor.

**Day 10 — Žabljak.** Check mountain conditions; some routes are exposed and strenuous.

**Day 11 — Žabljak.** Use as weather buffer if Day 10 was canceled.

**Day 12 — Kotor.** Budget most of a half day for transfers.

**Day 13 — Kotor.** The fortress ascent is strenuous in heat.

**Day 14 — Kotor.** A small boat trip beats a long mass-market cruise.

**Day 15 — Kotor.** Optional Budva is lower priority than Lovćen.

**Day 16 — Ulcinj.** Use Ulcinj to avoid extra Kotor→Shkodër transfer fatigue.

**Day 17 — Shkodër.** Carry cash: no ATMs in the alpine villages.

**Day 18 — Valbonë.** Reserve the ferry and onward transfer; operator publishes seasonal schedule.

**Day 19 — Theth.** Carry water, layers; do not start in thunderstorms.

**Day 20 — Theth.** Bad-weather buffer after high pass; not a needless filler day.

**Day 21 — Tirana.** Avoid booking anything with a strict arrival hour.

**Day 22 — Berat.** One partial day in Tirana is sufficient for this route.

**Day 23 — Berat.** Do not promise Osum whitewater rafting in September: water can be low.

**Day 24 — Përmet.** This stop can be cut to gain beach time.

**Day 25 — Gjirokastër.** Allow hills and steps; stay in or near the historic core.

**Day 26 — Himarë.** Choose Gjipe if time/sea state allows; don't cram both Gjipe and Porto Palermo.

**Day 27 — Corfu.** Do not rely on an unprotected same-day flight connection.

**Day 28 — Home.** Keep final-day international air buffer; flight is a separate booking.

## Buffers, transport constraints and reservations

- **Border travel:** plan +1–2 hours contingency on international road days, especially summer weekends. Check each bus operator and exact departure.
- **Plužine/Žabljak:** a through bus is not established. Sarajevo→Plužine coaches have been advertised; a local prearranged road transfer is key, especially for the scenic Durmitor Ring. The Sedlo pass road is narrow and weather-dependent.
- **Koman ferry:** operator's 2026 season 15 April–5 November, departures Koman 09:00 and Fierzë 13:00; confirm 2027 before bookings. Operator shuttle links from Shkodër operate early.
- **Valbonë–Theth:** strenuous full-day alpine hike; accommodation on each side, check rain/lightning and carry enough cash. If poor conditions, do not attempt to force the hike. There is no direct car road across the pass.
- **September Osum rafting:** not a reliable whitewater promise; canyon hiking/views only where conditions are suitable.
- **Corfu exit:** ferry + border immigration can take longer than sailing time. The plan intentionally sleeps in Corfu the night before an air departure.
- **Luggage:** hike with a daypack; arrange luggage storage/transport in Shkodër if repeating the same accommodation, or pay for cross-valley luggage transfer.
