# Speedrun — all important S/A sights in minimum feasible dwell time

**Theoretical minimum: ~18 days** with near-perfect timing, a private Plužine transfer, reliable weather and efficiently aligned ferries. **Recommended Speedrun: 19–20 days** (19-day skeleton below + an optional extra weather/transport protection day in Durmitor or Valbonë/Theth). This is not a relaxed itinerary.

The Speedrun has the *same broad geography* as the full itinerary and therefore reuses the [full route map](maps/map-full-route.svg). It cuts slack and some B/C sights, not Sarajevo, Durmitor or the major Albanian Alps hike.

**Protected extra day:** if hiking is non-negotiable, take **20 days** and place the movable day at Žabljak first; if alpine weather forecasts look worse around Valbonë, move it to the Theth/Valbonë segment. Do not plan both major hikes without recovery.

## Why not 14–15 days?

Two strenuous mountain systems, two border crossings, a sea crossing, and cross-country buses with infrequent departures cannot reliably fit into 14 days *while retaining essentially all S/A sights*. The 12-day 60:40 plan intentionally drops Sarajevo and Durmitor; therefore it is **not** a Speedrun.

## Bookings to avoid a fake Speedrun

1. Confirm the flight **and arrival time** before specifying Day 1.
2. Check Sarajevo→Plužine coach and reserve **Plužine→Žabljak** pickup in advance. A bus to Žabljak is *not guaranteed*.
3. Book the Koman departure and local shuttles; do not construct missed-connection itineraries.
4. Only set the high pass on a suitable weather day; no crossing during storms/snow.
5. Never connect the evening Albania ferry directly to a nonrefundable same-day international flight.

Opening times are placeholders: an '08:30 museum' stop requires checking the attraction's operating day and last entry. See [dated booking checks](booking-example-2027-09.md).
