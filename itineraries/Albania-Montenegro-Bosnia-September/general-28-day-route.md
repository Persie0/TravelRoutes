# General optimal 28-day first-trip route

The full trip deliberately devotes 7 days to Bosnia's Herzegovina/Neretva/Sarajevo cluster, 9 days to Montenegro's Durmitor+Kotor/coast region, and 11 days to Albania's mountains/UNESCO/Riviera, plus a Greek exit day. Counts overlap on transfers and are approximate.

**Direction for September:** Dubrovnik Airport (gateway) → Mostar → Konjic → Sarajevo → Plužine / Piva Lake → Durmitor / Žabljak → Bay of Kotor → Ulcinj → Shkodër → Koman Lake ferry → Valbonë → Theth → Tirana → Berat → Përmet → Gjirokastër → Himarë → Butrint / Sarandë → Corfu Airport.

The route is adapted to the season: do higher and wetter hikes before the end of September, then beach-hop south; preserving Corfu as the *exit* avoids a 5–7 h return to Tirana from the far-southern coast.

- **Signature scenery:** Durmitor, Bay of Kotor, Koman ferry and Valbonë–Theth pass.
- **Signature culture:** Mostar, Sarajevo, Berat, Gjirokastër and Butrint.
- **High-value active days:** Neretva rafting, Durmitor mountain trek, Kotor fortress, Koman ferry, Albanian Alps crossing, Gjipe.
- **The real bottleneck:** Sarajevo→Plužine→Žabljak. Favor a reserved Plužine driver + road transfer; public-only option via Nikšić may consume a full day or require overnight.
- **Avoid:** Sarajevo→Mostar→Kotor→Žabljak→back south when using a car; it repeats long distances. If strictly public transport and no private legs, that alternate may be more dependable.
- **Austrian passport:** visas not normally needed for short tourism stays in Bosnia, Montenegro or Albania. Check passport validity at least 3 months after departure and current BMEIA advice.
