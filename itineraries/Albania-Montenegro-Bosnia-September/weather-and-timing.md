## Which dates to choose?

**Best overall: September.** High-mountain paths and the Koman boat generally operate; inland town walks are more comfortable than in August; the Riviera sea still retains summer heat.

**Second-best: late June into early July.** Good high-altitude hikes after the snow season and a well-running ferry/coast season; hotter cities and beaches than September. **Best-value shoulder:** mid/late September after the busiest coast period, but check businesses' late-season closures.

**Avoid for this exact full route:** November–April; high alpine hiking may be unsafe/inaccessible, and Koman ferry road/ferry service is seasonal. May is good for heritage but too early for a normal Valbonë–Theth high pass crossing. July/August are hike-friendly but hotter, busier and typically costlier on the Adriatic/Ionian coasts.

### Typical September conditions (climate averages / planning context)

- **Sarajevo:** World Meteorological Organization climatology lists September daytime mean high ~**22.5°C**, mean low ~10.4°C (1991–2020), with rain still possible. Mostar is often warmer.
- **Theth:** one sourced guide reports around **17°C daytime**, **9°C nighttime** in September, and wetter conditions than Tirana; a pass at nearly 1,800 m can be much colder/windier.
- **Montenegro coast / Kotor:** 2026 comparative guide suggests about **25°C daytime** and sea temperature around **24°C** in September, with autumn showers beginning.
- **South Albania / Sarandë:** a September comparison based on multiple sea datasets places sea temperature roughly **24.5–25.2°C**, while typical afternoons may reach the high 20s. Ideal for swimming, not a promise of daily sunshine.

### September ordering inside this route

Start **1–4 Sep in Bosnia**; try to complete **Durmitor days 8–11 Sep** and the **Valbonë–Theth crossing 19 Sep**. The mountain itinerary is earlier than the coast (days 25–27 Sep). This is deliberate, although peak hiking weather is not guaranteed on those specific dates. Book a flexible mountain guesthouse arrangement if possible.

### Speedrun weather failure points

1. **Prutaš/Bobotov Kuk:** wind/low cloud/rain: substitute Black Lake/Ćurevac. A one-day summit slot is brittle.
2. **Valbonë–Theth:** sustained rain, lightning, snow, wind or route closure require canceling the pass. A full-day alternate road detour is *not* a quick replacement.
3. **Koman ferry:** seasonal schedule does not rule out local disruption; confirm with the operator the day before.
4. **Sarandë–Corfu ferry:** sea condition and immigration delays; plan a flight on the **following day** when possible.
5. **Osum canyon and Neretva rafting:** water levels and operator safety vary, not equal all-season rafting.

**Pack:** light waterproof shell and trousers, warm layer/hat, grippy hiking shoes, dry bag, sun protection, offline maps, poles if helpful, swimsuits. The same trip includes mountain nights and warm Ionian beaches.
