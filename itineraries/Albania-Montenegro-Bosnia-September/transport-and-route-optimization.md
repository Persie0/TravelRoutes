# Transport and aggressive route optimization

Research snapshot: **8 November 2026**. All times and prices below are *indicative planning bands* based on referenced 2026 services unless an operator explicitly confirmed them. No 2027 bus/ferry schedules have been verified; check again before paying.

## Winning direction and competing shells

**Recommended:** fly Vienna→Dubrovnik, transfer directly to **Mostar**, continue **Konjic→Sarajevo**, cross near **Foča/Plužine** into Durmitor, then **Kotor→Ulcinj→Shkodër**, finish the Albanian north-to-south route and take **Sarandë→Corfu ferry** for the flight home.

Why it wins: competitive Dubrovnik entry fare; Bosnia's historic/Neretva cluster early; Montenegro's inland mountains before coast; Albanian ferry/hiking circuit flows via Shkodër; Albanian UNESCO towns lead to Riviera; Corfu avoids backtracking to Tirana.

### The critical fork after Sarajevo

**Geographically optimal (requires one booked transfer):** Sarajevo/East Sarajevo→Plužine by coach via border; prebook driver/road leg to Žabljak. Add lakeside viewpoints and Sedlo/Durmitor Ring if accessible. **Do not market this as a guaranteed through-coach itinerary.** Rome2Rio indicates East Sarajevo→Plužine services on some dates; **Plužine→Žabljak is not a confirmed conventional public-bus connection**. Build a 1-day contingency, not an hour-by-hour fantasy.

**Public-transport fallback:** Sarajevo→Mostar (~2 h scenic rail), Mostar→Kotor (~4–6 h+), Kotor→Žabljak via Nikšić (~3.5–5.5 h), then Žabljak→Podgorica/Ulcinj→Shkodër. This sacrifices several hours of backtracking but has better-established intercity-bus legs. Another option is Sarajevo→Foča→Nikšić and a timed Žabljak coach; confirm transfer feasibility and overnight need. The routes through Foča and Nikšić use **separate tickets** unless clearly sold as a through service.

## What to book / when

1. **Flights first, but as an open-jaw pair.** Confirm arrival before committing a same-day Bosnia transfer.
2. **Koman ferry and connecting shuttles** at [the operator](https://komanilakeferry.com/timetables-and-prices/); official 2026 Berisha season: 15 Apr–5 Nov, Koman 09:00 and Fierzë 13:00.
3. **Žabljak transfer** from Plužine before leaving Sarajevo; keep a public-only contingency.
4. **Valbonë and Theth guesthouses** and luggage plan, plus forecast.
5. **Corfu ferry** at [Ionian Seaways](https://ionianseaways.com/sq/orari/) or [Finikas](https://www.ferries.gr/en/ferry-companies/finikas-lines/route/corfu-saranda/), selecting an actual 2027 timetable.
6. **Key local activities:** licensed rafting operators and mountain guides only when required by trail skill/conditions; independent hiking otherwise.

## Car vs no car

For a solo or small-group budget traveler, **coach + minibus + 2–4 locally hired road-transfer/daytrip legs** is often better value than a single Croatia→Bosnia→Montenegro→Albania→Greece one-way rental. Cross-border car hire needs written permission, insurance cover and possibly large one-way fees; a Corfu-bound rental car can also be barred by ferry companies. For two or more travelers, local short rentals/driver split costs are attractive around Durmitor, Lovćen and Riviera.

**Do not assume rail connects these countries.** Use the scenic Sarajevo–Mostar railway if it fits, but buses are the international backbone.
