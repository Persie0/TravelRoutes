## Map / geographic provenance

**Source coordinates** in [maps/stops.geojson](maps/stops.geojson) use GeoJSON **[longitude, latitude]**; where precision matters (entry gate/harbor rather than town centroid), re-geocode the exact location before using them for navigation. Albanian places were cross-checked against the [Visit Albania atlas](https://visitalbania.guide/map); Plužine location checked against [Mapcarta/OSM](https://mapcarta.com/N791129565) (~43.157N,18.844E). Other city/park centres were checked against common gazetteer and cartographic location references.

**Static maps** are based on true **GSHHS low-resolution coastlines and country borders** (via Basemap cartographic geometry), projected to a fixed regional view. They include geographical border and coastline vectors, unlike illustrative AI drawings. Route GeoJSON lines are **straight planning connectors**, not exact winding roads, trails or ferry tracks. The reusable [map generator](../../tools/python/script.py) reads GeoJSON/map-config and can regenerate SVGs from Basemap, offering an independent cartographic rendering.

[OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) attribution applies to the locations/mapping references and the interactive Leaflet map. Low-resolution GSHHS/Basemap coastlines and borders are attributed on each SVG.

## Limitations

No 2027 timetable/real-time inventory has been confirmed for ferries, buses, hotels, activities or flights. Date-specific air prices are **Skyscanner estimates**, not guaranteed sellable seats. Private transfer time/cost ranges are explicitly unquoted planning estimates. Weather scores in [weather-and-timing.md](weather-and-timing.md) are editorial activity-suitability estimates, not meteorological model outputs. Sight rankings and 60:40 capture are editorial and may not match an individual's tastes.
