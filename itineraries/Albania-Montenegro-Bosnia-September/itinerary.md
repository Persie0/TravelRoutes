## Hard booking dependencies

- **Flight 1 September** arrival must allow border transfer and Mostar same day. If not, start Sep 2 and adjust the dated schedule, rather than forcing a midnight transfer.
- **8 September** Sarajevo→Plužine→Žabljak requires service confirmation and a reserved last-mile driver; if not viable, use [transport alternative](transport-and-route-optimization.md) with a change near Mostar/Kotor/Nikšić.
- **18 September** shuttle and Koman 09:00 ferry, and the Fierzë→Valbonë onward transfer must all operate on that 2027 day.
- **19 September** alpine high pass hike **only in safe weather**. Recovery/replan time on Sep 20.
- **27 September** Sarandë→Corfu international ferry must arrive on the island **the evening before** Sep 28 flight.
- **28 September** airport buffer, including time from Corfu town/port; the sampled flight fare does not establish departure time.
