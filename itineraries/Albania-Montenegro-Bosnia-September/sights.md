# Sight catalogue and editorial score — September Balkans

Each sight has **priority S/A/B/C**, **sightseeing value /10**, **uniqueness /10**, **time cost**, **detour**, and a concise reason. Scores are **explicit planning judgments**, not official ratings; prices/opening hours require reconfirmation. The complete machine-readable dataset is [data/sights.csv](data/sights.csv) (55 locations/experiences).

**Method:** score per sight = `(0.65 × value + 0.35 × uniqueness) × tier-weight` where S=1.5, A=1.2, B=0.8, C=0.4. The model is not a substitute for route geometry and weather; it deliberately does not treat 100 scores as 100% visitor satisfaction.

**Full core score:** 417.7 from 40 core sights. **60:40 selected core sights:** 241.3 from 20 sights, **57.8%** of the additive editorial core score. The 60:40 visit count includes stops that may be combined within one cluster or done briefly; actual personally-realized share varies with weather and the specific optional choices.

## Prioritization notes

- **S-tier non-negotiables on the full route:** Stari Most, Sarajevo old town, Durmitor Black Lake, Kotor old town & fortress, Koman ferry, Valbonë–Theth pass, Berat, Gjirokastër, Gjipe and Butrint.
- **High time-cost sights:** Bobotov Kuk, additional rafting days, Prokletije extension, Osum Canyon from Berat. Some are spectacular but lose against the three-country route geometry.
- **Beaches:** Himarë/Livadhi/Gjipe outrank an extra day in overcrowded Ksamil for varied scenery.
- **No solo trek shortcut:** Prutaš and Bobotov Kuk are *alternatives* in the full version, not mandatory back-to-back peaks.
- **Season constraint:** September is not the default whitewater season for Osum. Consider Neretva rafting or coastal activities instead.
- **Access:** no public transport assumed to every canyon/peak/remote thermal pool. All require a real local transfer plan.
