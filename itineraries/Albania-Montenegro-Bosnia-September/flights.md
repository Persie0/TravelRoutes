### Raw cheapest airfare vs best value

The lowest **independent monthly one-way observations** across all tested starts/ends are not necessarily compatible with the exact same 28-day trip; do not sum different dates or reverse-direction journeys and present that as a real itinerary quote. A return through Tirana (example 27 Sep €84) can beat CFU (€110) on raw air fare **by €26**, but requires a several-hour reposition from Sarandë to Tirana and likely loses a sightseeing day. The CFU option adds ferry and one Corfu overnight, so **the full cash delta is not always in CFU's favor**. For a nature-/coast-first trip, time and energy favor CFU if flight times work.

1. **VIE→DBV // CFU→VIE (recommended full route).** Minimal end-of-trip Albania backtracking; pair €148 observed for 1/28 Sep before add-ons.
2. **VIE→DBV // TIA→VIE.** Typically lower flight-only price possible, but move from Sarandë to Tirana takes roughly 5–7 hours; avoid same-day tight airport connection.
3. **VIE→SJJ // CFU→VIE.** More natural Sarajevo start and saves the Dubrovnik→Mostar airport transfer, but September 1 observed connecting SJJ fare is much higher; compare again when actual flight inventory changes.
4. **VIE→TIA // CFU→VIE, reversed route.** Start north-Albania positioning, then revisit Albania south after Bosnia/Montenegro, or add costly zigzag. Bad for the complete route, though excellent for Albania-only trips.
5. **BUD→TIA // CFU→BUD or VIE.** Some cheap budget fares (€32 outbound 4 Sep, €61 CFU→BUD 29 Sep), but Vienna–Budapest ground travel and the wrong route direction can erase savings.
6. **VIE→TGD or DBV // TIA/CFU→VIE.** Worth checking only if a short Montenegro/Bosnia-first trip is prioritized.
