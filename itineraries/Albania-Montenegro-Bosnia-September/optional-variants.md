# Useful variants (not extra documents for their own sake)

## A. My default: nature / activity-first full route, 28 days

Exactly the [28-day itinerary](itinerary-28-days.md). Active anchors: Neretva rafting, Durmitor day hike, Kotor fortress, Koman ferry + Valbonë–Theth pass, Gjipe Beach. Limit city sightseeing to Mostar/Sarajevo/Tirana heritage clusters, rather than collecting museums.

## B. Public-transport-only (low logistical risk)

Reorder Montenegro to use better-supported buses:

**Mostar → Sarajevo → train back Mostar → Kotor → Žabljak (return through Podgorica/Nikšić) → Ulcinj → Shkodër → Albania.**

Cost: potential extra **1–2 transit days** or cutting an optional beach/rest day. Reward: avoids dependence on a private Plužine→Žabljak pickup. It may be cheaper for a solo traveler who cannot share transfers. Verify each bus connection and don't assume nightly service.

## C. Hiking-heavy (+3–5 days)

Add one of (not both):
- **Montenegro Prokletije/Grebaje** 2–3 nights near Gusinje with remote trails; mountain road/bus geometry makes this a genuine detour.
- **Longer Albanian Alps** one extra Valbonë night + two in Theth for Blue Eye, waterfall and a more ambitious valley trail, plus forecast buffer.

High-value for hikers, but avoid late October.

## D. Coastal / beach-heavy (same 28 days)

Cut Konjic rafting, the third Kotor day, a Durmitor extra activity day and Përmet. Reallocate 3–4 days to **Himarë / Dhërmi / Gjipe / Ksamil**. This is best if beach swimming is higher priority than second-tier heritage.

## E. Budget / hostels

Prefer intercity coach, Shkodër-based ferry/guesthouse arrangements, two-bed dorms and small rooms; share driver days from Plužine and for Lovćen. The biggest cost red flags are **three-country one-way car rental**, late-booked Bay of Kotor lodging and individually booked taxis between Albanian towns.

Very rough **28-day per-person planning envelopes**, *not verified quotes*: **€1,300–2,100 budget solo** including flight/food/dorms/local buses and some activities, **€2,100–3,300 private-room comfort** including more shared/private road transfers. Highly sensitive to lodging, rafting fees, luggage and airline fare. Albania-only backpacker pricing can be around €35–50/day, but Kotor/Corfu and the mountain transfers cost more.

## F. Short trip, ~12 days

Use the separate [60:40 itinerary](itinerary-60-40.md); preserve the spectacular Koman ferry/pass, Kotor and southern Albania, but consciously **drop Sarajevo/Durmitor**. This is not the right variant if Durmitor is your top priority.

## G. Notable extensions (if extra time)

- **Dubrovnik old town**: 1 day; airport is used anyway, but expensive/overcrowded relative to Bosnia/Montenegro core.
- **Corfu**: add 2–4 days if you want a Greek island beach ending instead of only using its airport.
- **Ohrid, North Macedonia**: beautiful UNESCO lake region, but a geographically awkward eastern detour for this west-Balkans spine. Add only if extending ≥4 days.

## H. Alternate airport finish

**Tirana rather than Corfu:** save sea crossing and Corfu accommodation but spend roughly 5–7 hours returning from Sarandë to TIA. Consider only if the flight is materially cheaper or ferry weather is forecast poor. See [flights](flights.md).
