## Pacing notes

- The four Lofoten days are deliberate: weather can wipe out one hiking day.
- Do **not** force Trollstigen into the route. If it is confirmed open for your year, it becomes an optional detour around Days 16–18.
- If you want **Trolltunga**, add two days around Odda (one weather-buffer day + one hike day). Do not squeeze it into Day 28.
- For a strict 28-day trip, cut Day 14 Trondheim and either Day 17 Ålesund or Day 23 Balestrand.
