# Worthwhile variants

## A. Maximum scenic-road version — 32–34 days

Replace Bodø→Trondheim train with **Helgelandskysten (Fv17)**. Add Svartisen/Engabreen area, Sandnessjøen/Seven Sisters and Torghatten. Best if driving itself is a core goal.

## B. Hiking-heavy — 32 days

Add:
- Senja: extra weather day for Hesten/Segla;
- Lofoten: Ryten/Kvalvika + Reinebringen;
- Loen: Via Ferrata or long ridge hike;
- **Trolltunga:** 2 days around Odda;
- Preikestolen early morning.

## C. 28-day strict version

Cut:
- Trondheim full city day;
- one of Ålesund full day / Balestrand full day.

Do not cut Lofoten weather buffer or Geiranger/Loen below three combined days.

## D. September version — reverse direction

**Stavanger → Bergen → Sognefjord → Loen/Geiranger → Ålesund → Trondheim → train Bodø → Lofoten → Senja → Tromsø.**

Reason: do high seasonal mountain roads first, finish north for darker skies/aurora. If Gryllefjord–Andenes ferry has ended, route Lofoten → Evenes/Narvik → Senja by road instead.

## E. Cheaper car version

If TOS→BOD one-way is punitive:
- fly VIE→EVE (Evenes) if airfare works;
- rent Evenes loop for Vesterålen/Lofoten;
- skip Tromsø/Senja;
- ferry or fly onward from Bodø;
- this sacrifices 2 excellent areas but can cut rental logistics substantially.

## F. Camper/car-sleeping version

Works well in principle, but do not interpret the right to roam as permission to park anywhere. Use legal roadside parking/designated campsites, especially in Lofoten and around popular trailheads. A simple estate car can work for sleeping; a campervan gives more comfort but costs more on ferries and often in rental.
