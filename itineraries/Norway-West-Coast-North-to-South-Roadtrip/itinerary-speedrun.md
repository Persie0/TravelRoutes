# Speedrun itinerary — Norway west coast north → south

## Goal

Keep the important S/A-tier Arctic coast, Lofoten, fjords and major hikes, while deleting weather slack, duplicate scenic days and slow city time.

- Full route: 30 days
- 60:40: ~12 days, whole regions cut
- **Speedrun theoretical minimum: ~20 days**
- **Recommended Speedrun minimum: 22 days**

Two extra days are strongly recommended because Senja/Lofoten ferries, mountain roads, fjord weather and major hikes cannot be treated like guaranteed urban transit.

## Important experiences retained

Tromsø/Kvaløya, Senja, Andøya, Lofoten east/west + major hike, Moskenes ferry, Nordland Railway, Trondheim/Nidaros, Atlantic Road, Ålesund, Geiranger, Strynefjell/Loen, Sognefjord/Aurland, Nærøyfjord/Flåm, Bergen, Hardanger, Ryfylke and Preikestolen.

## Speedrun rules

1. Keep the hybrid rental-car + Nordland Railway structure; one continuous car is not automatically faster or cheaper.
2. Recheck Gryllefjord–Andenes and Moskenes–Bodø ferry schedules for the actual travel year.
3. Move Day 21 to whichever of Senja, Lofoten, Geiranger/Loen or Preikestolen gets bad weather.
4. Do not force Gamle Strynefjellsvegen or other seasonal roads if closed.
5. A 20-day theoretical run is created by deleting Day 21 and compressing Bergen/Hardanger further; that is substantially more brittle.

**Speedrun deletes duplicate fjord days and city buffers, not the north-to-south scenic spine.**
