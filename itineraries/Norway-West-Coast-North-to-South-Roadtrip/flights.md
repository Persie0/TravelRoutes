# Flight strategy

Research snapshot: **26 August 2026**, for a flexible 2027 summer trip.

## Best route shell

Use an **open-jaw** trip:

**Vienna (VIE) → Tromsø (TOS)**  
then road/train south  
**Stavanger (SVG) → Vienna (VIE)**

Do not backtrack to Tromsø or Oslo just to force a round trip.

## Indicative Skyscanner observations

These are **month-level indicative one-way fares**, not guaranteed bookable prices for the exact 30-day dates.

- **VIE → TOS, July 2027:** observed from about **€162** with connection; direct Austrian options shown much higher in the same snapshot.
- **VIE → TOS, August 2027:** observed from about **€200** with Finnair; direct Austrian shown from about **€654** in that snapshot.
- **BGO → VIE, August 2027 fallback:** observed from about **€132** with SAS; direct Austrian around **€158** on one quoted day.

Skyscanner had no usable indicative quote in the snapshot for **SVG → VIE August 2027**, so do not infer that the route is unavailable. Search the exact dates once schedules are fully loaded.

## Decision rule

1. Price **VIE→TOS + SVG→VIE** first.
2. Compare **VIE→TOS + BGO→VIE** only if Stavanger return is expensive.
3. If BGO is >~€100 cheaper after luggage **and** the rental return works, consider ending in Bergen and making Stavanger a separate extension.
4. Also compare a cheap **SVG→OSL** positioning flight plus **OSL→VIE**, but count connection risk and baggage twice.
5. Budapest is worth checking only after Vienna; do not add a 2.5–3 h ground reposition for a trivial saving.

Source attribution: Skyscanner indicative-prices tool; fares change continuously.
