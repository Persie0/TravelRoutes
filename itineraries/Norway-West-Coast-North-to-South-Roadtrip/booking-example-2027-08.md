# Worked booking example — August 2027

This is a planning layer, not a claim that every timetable is already published.

## Suggested date frame

**2 August → 31 August 2027** (30 days)

Direction: **north → south**.

## Flight shell

- Outbound target: **VIE → TOS**, 2 August 2027.
- Skyscanner indicative snapshot on 26 Aug 2026 showed **~€200 one-way** with Finnair for 2 Aug.
- Return target: **SVG → VIE**, 31 Aug 2027; re-search exact fare when inventory is mature.
- Bergen fallback: month-level August snapshot showed **BGO → VIE from ~€132** with SAS and a direct Austrian example around **€158** on an early-August day. This is a fallback benchmark, not a 31 Aug quote.

## Ground-booking order

1. Search northern rental **TOS → BOD** with all one-way fees.
2. Search southern rental **TRD → SVG**.
3. Compare against one-car **TOS → SVG** total.
4. Book the two seasonal bottleneck ferries as soon as the travel-year timetable is released where reservations apply.
5. Book **Bodø → Trondheim day train** after confirming full-rail operation.
6. Reserve the hardest accommodation nights: Reine/Hamnøy, Geiranger, Loen, Aurland.
7. Keep at least one Lofoten and one fjord day flexible for weather.

## If rental pricing is ugly

- Return northern car around Evenes/Svolvær and use passenger transport/ferry to Bodø.
- Or keep one car and **drive Helgelandskysten**, turning the transfer into a 2–3 day scenic segment.
- Or shorten the full trip to Bergen and make Stavanger/Preikestolen a future extension.
