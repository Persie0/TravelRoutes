## B-tier / conditional

Trondheim city, Saltstraumen, Flåm Railway, Stavanger city. All are worthwhile, but they should not displace the top nature days.

**Trollstigen:** A-tier only if the official road status is open and stable. It is not a structural dependency of this itinerary.
