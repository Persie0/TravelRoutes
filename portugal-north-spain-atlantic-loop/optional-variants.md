# Optional variants

## 1. Nature/hiking-heavy
Cut Coimbra + one Bilbao city day; add a second Picos weather-buffer/hike day, Fragas do Eume, Costa Quebrada west of Santander and Urdaibai/Gaztelugatxe coast.

## 2. Beach/surf-heavy
Add 2–3 days split between Algarve west coast (Arrifana/Amado), Galicia Rías Baixas, Asturias Llanes coast and Cantabria/Basque surf beaches. Cut Coimbra, Burgos and León city time.

## 3. City/culture-heavy
Add Braga/Guimarães from Porto, then Bilbao + a San Sebastián extension after Bilbao. This is longer but culturally stronger.

## 4. San Sebastián extension
Bilbao → San Sebastián → Bilbao/inland return adds ~1.5–2 days and geographic backtracking. Worth it for food/urban interest; not optimal for the stated coast-to-Bilbao-then-Porto loop.

## 5. Maximum-efficiency 28 days
Delete Faro day 2 and Porto day 29 buffer. Everything else stays.

## 6. Slower 35 days
Add one night each: Lagos, Lisbon, Santiago/Costa da Morte, Picos and Basque coast. This reduces the two most intense transfer chains substantially.
