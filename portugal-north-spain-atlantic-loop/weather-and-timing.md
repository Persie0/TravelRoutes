# Weather and timing

**September is one of the best compromise months for this exact mixed trip.** Spain’s official tourism site describes September as still summery but cooler and particularly good for hiking/touring; Asturias tourism says September is particularly good for mountain lovers because heat is lower while days remain long and bright. Northern Spain still has meaningful rain risk.

## Best timing
- **Best overall:** **September**.
- **Second-best:** June.
- **Best value shoulder:** late May / early June or late September.
- **Avoid for this exact mix:** roughly November–February if Picos hiking + beach time matter.

## September-specific risks
- Algarve can still be hot; do cliff walks early/late.
- Galicia/Asturias/Cantabria/Basque Country remain Atlantic: rain fronts can arrive any week.
- Picos conditions can change rapidly; never make a committing high route your only mountain plan.
- Sea/ferry conditions can affect Cíes.

Portugal’s tourism board notes the north is cooler than the south and that coastal influence moderates summer heat; the Algarve has very high annual sunshine. Spain’s tourism board notes northern summer weather is milder with some rain in Galicia, Asturias and Cantabria.
